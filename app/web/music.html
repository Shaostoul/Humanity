Music

The game composes its own music as it plays: a genre's rules and a number, the seed, make a piece. Explore the pieces, change them, keep the ones you love, and take them anywhere.

They work whenever you are not typing in a box. In the desktop app, Space, N, P and L also pause, skip and like the music it plays.

What plays

In the desktop app this page is the one place that chooses what the music plays: let the game choose, or your own genre, mood and seed, played on every page and in the world until you choose again. Settings > Music there sets only how the music sounds.

The desktop app plays the music here: what plays, its tuning, the piano roll and the mixer. Choose a genre and a mood under Explore and a written piece under Written music to see what each is; the app plays them.

The music is composed live by the app's own engine, which runs in the desktop app; the website cannot play it yet. This is the order we are building in, not a law, a patent or a platform rule: a later step compiles the music engine for the browser.

Genres

Each genre is a style with its own beat, instruments and feel. Click a card to choose it; it plays from the next bar.

Moods

A mood is where the music plays and how it feels there. The game picks one for each place; choosing one here makes it yours.

Pieces you opened here

The written pieces whose details you opened on this website, newest first, kept only in this browser. The desktop app keeps the pieces you heard the same way, in your data folder.

    Written music

    Music written down note by note, like a printed score, and played on the game's own instruments at your tuning. Each piece is a file anyone can open and change.
