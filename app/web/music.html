Music

The game composes its own music as it plays: a genre's rules and a number, the seed, make a piece. Explore the pieces, change them, keep the ones you love, and take them anywhere.

The music is composed live by the app's own engine, which runs in the desktop app; the website cannot play it yet. This is the order we are building in, not a law, a patent or a platform rule: a later step compiles the music engine for the browser.
