Member Directory

On: you are in this server's member directory and its search, and a signed copy of your profile (without what you keep for friends) is kept by this server and passed to the servers it is linked with, where anyone can look it up by your public key. Off: you are left out of the directory and its search, every linked server drops its copy, and looking up your key finds nothing. It is off until you turn it on. Off does not hide your name or your profile from the people on a server you use.
