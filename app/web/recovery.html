Recovery through trusted people

Part of Settings, beside your recovery phrase.

What it is

If you ever lose this device and your recovery phrase too, people you trust could help you back into your account. You would pick a few of them as your guardians. Each would keep one piece of your recovery phrase, locked so that only that guardian can open it, and the server would hold the pieces without being able to read them. One piece alone is not enough: it takes as many of your guardians as you chose, together, to put the phrase back together, and with it you are back in the same account.

Not finished yet. Choosing your guardians and splitting your phrase into pieces has not been built, so nobody can set this up today. Until it is, your recovery phrase is the only way back into your account: keep it written down somewhere safe.

What works today is looking things up on this server: whether an account has guardians, and the pieces a guardian keeps for other people. An account ID starts with did:hum:; the desktop app shows yours in Settings > Account.
