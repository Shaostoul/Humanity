Recovery through trusted people

Part of Settings, beside your recovery phrase.

What it is

If you ever lose your device and your recovery phrase too, people you trust can help you back into your account. You choose a few of your friends. Each keeps one piece of your recovery phrase, locked so that only they can open it. The server keeps the locked pieces without being able to read them, and it is never told who your trusted people are. One piece alone is no use to anyone: it takes as many of them as you chose, together, to put your phrase back together, and with it you are back in the same account.

Choose people who know your voice and your face. Anyone who wants into your account would have to pretend to be you to them, so they should always check by calling you or seeing you before they help.

Your recovery phrase is still the main way back in: keep it written down somewhere safe. Trusted people are for when that is lost too.

Your trusted people

Choose from your friends (people you follow who follow you back), between 2 and 10 of them.

Fewer is easier if someone is hard to reach. More is safer if one of them is ever fooled. Changing it later sends new pieces, and the old ones stop counting on the server.

Help someone get back in

Before you send anything, make sure it is really them. Call them on a number you already had, or see them in person. Do not trust a text, an email or a message from a new account on its own: someone who wants into their account would send exactly that. Ask them to read you the code their new device shows.

Your piece goes only to their new device, locked so that nobody else can open it, the server included.

Lost your device? Ask your trusted people for help

Use this on your new device. Keep this page open until enough of your trusted people have answered (a request lasts 7 days; closing the page means asking again with a new code).

Call each of your trusted people, or see them in person, and read them this code. They check it is really you, type it in, and send their piece to this device. Nobody but this device can open what they send.

Someone with you can scan the picture instead: it opens this page on their phone with the code filled in.

Your account is back together. Restore it in this browser, then write down your recovery phrase again (in Chat: Settings, or the /recovery command) and keep it somewhere safe.
