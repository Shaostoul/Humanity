Shared Files

Real, useful files shared by people on this server: 3D-printable parts, models, and designs. Download them, print them, improve them, share them back.

How to share your own: press Upload a file, or drop files on this page. Files here are public: anyone can download them. In the chat, 3D and model files you attach (.blend, .stl, .obj, .gltf, .glb) are listed here too; photos and other chat attachments stay private to the people you send them to. Sharing requires a verified account (ask an admin in chat). A file of yours here can also go on the Media page: press Put on Media on its row.
