Welcome to HumanityOS

A free platform where anyone can learn to provide for themselves, share what they know, trade and build together. No ads, no email or password, no owner. Pick a name and go straight into the community.

Your name is just a label other members see: no password, no email, no profile to fill in. Your computer makes a private signing key so your messages prove they are yours, and the key never leaves this device. Next you are shown a 24-word recovery phrase: that phrase is your account, and the only way back in if you lose this device, so write it down when it appears.
