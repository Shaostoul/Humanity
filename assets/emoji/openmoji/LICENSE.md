(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode>). They are NOT covered by the
project's own licences in `LICENSING.md` (AGPL-3.0 code, CC BY-SA 4.0 docs, CC0 data): they stay
OpenMoji's, under OpenMoji's licence.

- **Unchanged.** The drawings are OpenMoji's as released. Two files had a garbled word inside an
  `id` attribute repaired (nothing drawn changed); `SOURCE.md` names them, with the release and the
  zip's checksum.
- **If you pass them on** (a fork, a copy of the website, a download), keep this file with them:
  the credit above, the licence link, and the note of what was changed. The licence comes with no
  warranty (its section 5).
- **If you change a picture**, your changed picture is an adaptation and must be shared under
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (or a licence Creative Commons lists as compatible), credited to OpenMoji, saying
  what you changed. HumanityOS's own volunteer-drawn emoji are drawn from scratch for exactly this
  reason, so they can be public domain: `docs/reference/findings/2026-11-20-openmoji.md`.

Where this summary and the licence text disagree, the licence governs. Written by developers, not
