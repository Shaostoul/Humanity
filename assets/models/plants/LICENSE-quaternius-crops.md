# Quaternius Nature Crops Pack (CC0)

The `carrot_*`, `tomato_*`, and other low-poly crop model folders here are
converted from the **Nature Crops Pack** by **Quaternius**
(https://quaternius.com), released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public
domain dedication - https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- Source download: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-crops-pack
  (mirror of the author's pack; OBJ format)
- Conversion: `scripts/obj-to-plant-gltf.js` (OBJ+MTL to single-primitive
  glTF with a palette texture carrying the material colors)
- No attribution is required by CC0; this file credits the author anyway
  because being remembered is the point. Thank you, Quaternius.
