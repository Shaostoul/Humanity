# pail_screwTop_white_glossy_a01

Based on `pail_screwTop_white_glossy_a01` from NVIDIA SimReady Assets (https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/simready-assets), package
`simready.hf.nvidia.simready-assets.pail_screwTop_white_glossy_a01.2026.10.09_231023013297`, by NVIDIA, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Converted and modified by HumanityOS with `just simready` (scripts/simready/):

- converted from OpenUSD to binary glTF;
- every part joined into one mesh and simplified from 37022 to 2999 triangles, with a 749-triangle distance version;
- its materials baked into one texture set (base colour 512 px, normal and ORM 512 px) on a new UV layout;
- moved so its footprint is centred on the origin and its base sits on the floor;
- convex collider hulls computed from its collision parts, and its physics values carried into physics.ron.
