# safety_firstAidKit_box_plastic_a01

Based on `safety_firstAidKit_box_plastic_a01` from NVIDIA SimReady Assets (https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/simready-assets), package
`simready.hf.nvidia.simready-assets.safety_firstAidKit_box_plastic_a01.2026.10.09_155241226155`, by NVIDIA, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Converted and modified by HumanityOS with `just simready` (scripts/simready/):

- converted from OpenUSD to binary glTF;
- every part joined into one mesh and simplified from 15514 to 10000 triangles, with a 2500-triangle distance version;
- its materials baked into one texture set (base colour 1024 px, normal and ORM 512 px) on a new UV layout;
- moved so its footprint is centred on the origin and its base sits on the floor, and turned 90 degrees about the vertical;
- convex collider hulls computed from its collision parts, and its physics values carried into physics.ron;
- repainted: its label carried a white cross on a red disc in a red ring, a red-and-white cross emblem of the kind protected in law in many countries: every red turned to the green of the ISO 7010 first aid sign, giving a white cross on green; the words FIRST AID KIT, Dressings, Wound Cleaning, Burn Treating, Miscellaneous kept.
