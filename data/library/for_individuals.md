# HumanityOS for You

You have probably noticed that everything costs more than it used to, and
that your pay does not stretch the way it should. Growing your own food or
fixing your own things sounds nice, but nobody ever taught you how, and
learning feels like one more thing you do not have time for. Meanwhile
every app on your phone wants an account, an email, a subscription, your
attention, or your data.

HumanityOS is a free set of tools built for exactly that situation. It is
made by a small open project, not a company. There is nothing to buy,
nothing to sign up for, and no catch to find later. Parts of it are still
rough, and we will tell you which parts. Here is what it can do for you
right now.

## What you get on day one

**Learn one real skill, starting small.** You do not need land or money.
One tomato plant in a container on a windowsill is a real beginning, and
it teaches honestly: it lives or dies by what you actually do, not by
what you meant to do. The built-in library has step-by-step guides
written for people starting from zero, with every number sourced from
real agricultural authorities: your first tomato, storing water safely,
your first compost. The app helps you plan it and ask others when it
wilts.

**Talk to people, privately, with no signup.** The chat works the moment
you open it. No email, no phone number, no account. Private messages are
encrypted on your device before they are sent, so nobody in the middle can
read them, including us.

**Make a plan you can actually finish.** Tasks and notes turn a vague hope
like "grow some of my own food" into small steps you can check off, and a
private record of what worked, so what you learn this year is still yours
next year.

**See where you are.** Maps let you zoom from the whole galaxy down to
your own street. We are building toward maps that also show gardens,
tools, and people near you, because providing for yourself is easier
with neighbors, and cooperation is easiest when it is local.

**Trade without a middleman.** The marketplace lets you list what you have
and find what you need, person to person. No company sits in the middle
taking a cut, so the value stays with the people trading. The network is
young, so listings grow as people join.

## Why this is different from every other app

Most free apps are not free. You pay with your attention, your data, or an
account that can be suspended. HumanityOS is built the opposite way:

- No account. Your identity is a key that lives on your own device, backed
  up by a list of 24 ordinary words you write down and keep. No company
  holds it, so no company can take it away.
- No ads, no tracking, nothing harvested. There is nothing to sell you and
  no advertiser to serve.
- Nothing is for sale. The whole project is released into the public
  domain, which means it legally belongs to everyone, forever. It can
  never be bought up, locked down, or rented back to you.
- You can leave any time. Your identity and your private notes live on
  your device, and your key, the thing that proves you are you, never
  leaves it. If you walk away tomorrow, no company can hold anything
  hostage.

One honest note about money, since every project should say this plainly:
there is no company and no nonprofit behind HumanityOS. If you ever choose
to support it, that support is a personal gift to the person who maintains
it. It is not a purchase, not an investment, and not tax-deductible. Nobody
is required to give anything, ever.

## You do not need to be technical

This is built for people who do not think of themselves as computer
people.

- Every unusual term has a plain-language definition built in. If a word
  confuses you, the dictionary is one tap away.
- The guides assume no background. They start at the actual beginning.
- The community answers questions. Asking "what does this mean" or "my
  plant is dying, help" is a normal, welcome thing to do. The community
  is small and early; today the Discord is where people answer fastest,
  and a reply in the app's chat may take a while.
- If something confuses you or breaks, telling us is genuinely helpful.
  This project is early and built in the open. Some of it is half-built,
  some is rough, and some will break. Your confusion points at the exact
  spot we need to fix.

## About the game

HumanityOS includes a simulation game where you will practice the same
skills, growing food, managing water and power, running a homestead, in a
world where mistakes cost nothing. We want to be straight with you: the
game is still in development and not fully playable yet. The everyday
tools above, the chat, the plans, the maps, the marketplace, and the
library, are the real value today. The game is coming, and you can watch
it being built.

## Start small

You do not have to commit to anything. The smallest honest first step is
to visit the site, open the chat, and say hello. No signup stands in your
way, so hello takes about thirty seconds. The website tools (chat, plans,
maps, the library) work in the browser on your phone; the downloadable
app is for computers. If you want a step beyond hello, read one short
guide about one tomato plant. That is enough. Everything else can come
later, or never, and either way the tools stay free and stay yours.
