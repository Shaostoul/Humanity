# 2026-07-26: two solved mysteries and an honest-UI day

Eight releases, v0.974 through v0.981. The through-line: verify before
implementing. Two queue items dissolved under investigation into deeper,
older bugs (both now fixed), and two more turned out already-shipped.

## The invisible clouds (v0.974, BUG-048)

The queue item said "polish cloud underside banding." Probing found there
was NO underside to band: three ground look-ups (one under an active Rain
HUD) showed zero cloud deck overhead while orbit captures showed dense
cover. The v0.958 horizon-slab fade used absolute slant thresholds
(30-80 km) written for an assumed 2 km deck, but the drawn shell sits at
51 km - even the zenith fragment was 40 percent faded, everything below
~50 degrees elevation gone. Players saw cloud shadows sweep the ground
under a clear sky for 16 releases. Fixed with a grazing-RATIO fade
(slant / camera-to-shell gap, ~1/sin(elevation), dimensionless). Banding
itself: still unreproduced now that undersides render; re-judge under a
thick daylit deck before adding march jitter.

## Nameplates stop bleeding through walls (v0.975)

The homestead inc-1 note. The v0.429 room filter compared against a
containment box that was the WHOLE house, so all ten rooms' machine cards
showed through the partitions. Replaced with a true sightline test:
wall_collision grew SIGHT variants (window apertures open - glass blocks
walkers, not eyes) plus live closed doors; the HUD hides any card or crew
plate whose camera-to-anchor line crosses a blocker. Tab reveal stays.
Bonus honesty: outdoor machines now show cards outdoors, and the glass
sightline wall actually works for cards.

## The dark-grid mystery solved (v0.976)

Parked since v0.954 as a tiled-lighting mystery. It was never tiling:
Camera::uniforms() hardcodes light_count.x = 0, celestial_uniforms()
inherits it, and render_celestial_onto stamps it over the camera uniform -
and planet TERRAIN draws in the celestial pass. Every terrain fragment
looped zero point lights while the storage buffer sat full; ship interiors
lit because the scene pass rewrites the real count. One poke at offset 592
fixed it. A second bug hid under it: the v0.954 test grid used raw Y-up
camera.forward(), wrong in frame-locked surface mode - now derived from
the view matrix. Rig proof: 27 colored light pools on the night Sahara,
classic 89 FPS and tiled 87 FPS, A/B parity. First time point lights ever
lit planet terrain - campfires and headlights are now possible. Also
v0.976.2: the NPC crowd stress-test design note (knobs, counters, gate);
v0.976.3: the ocean-teleport altitude item closed as already-fixed
(v0.896 + v0.909.x, rig-verified).

## Grazing-angle ground smear (v0.977)

textureSampleLevel with an isotropic analytic LOD bypasses the sampler's
anisotropy entirely - that was the whole smear. ground_triplanar_grad now
passes true per-plane UV gradients to textureSampleGrad (aniso raised to
x8); gradients taken as fs_main's first statements (uniform-flow safe)
and rotated into the pinned domain exactly. A/B: mid-distance sand keeps
granular detail, FPS 86 to 92.

## Forage takes root (v0.978)

New "stationary" ai_behavior: a creature row that never moves - exempt
from the graze amble, idled by the AI at zero velocity. Berry Bush and
Wild Flax ship as renewable [E]-collect forage (the egg/milk/wool loop),
placed via wild_spawns.ron. Also fixed: wild-spawned renewable species
never got a Harvestable, so no wild spawn was collectable.

## The emitter pair (v0.979, v0.981)

Travel objectives could never advance - nothing emitted their events. Now
data/entities/destinations.ron names landmarks and the QuestSystem fires
"travel_<id>" edge-triggered on arrival; the exploration chain got its
Travel steps back. Talk followed in v0.981: opening a crew dialogue fires
"talk_<name-slug>". Every objective kind in the quest engine now advances.
The claimed "~80% dead quest ids" from the 2026-07-20 audit was stale (the
same-day rewrite fixed them); what ships instead is the permanent lockstep
test - every objective id and reward item in every shipped quest is
checked against its source-of-truth data file at build time.
