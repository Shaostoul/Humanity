# Code of conduct

This covers the HumanityOS project's own spaces: this GitHub repository (issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews), the project's Discord, and the project's server, united-humanity.us. It is
not a second rulebook. It applies the rules that server already publishes, so a contributor and a
member are held to the same words.

## The rules

The rules are the ones published at [united-humanity.us/rules](https://united-humanity.us/rules),
which sit under the [Humanity Accord](https://united-humanity.us/library). In short:

1. **Respect every person.** "No harassment, hate speech, doxxing or targeted attacks. Disagreement
   is fine. Contempt is not."
2. **Act in good faith.** Communicate honestly; no deception or manipulation.
3. **Protect privacy.** Do not share someone else's personal information without their consent.
4. **Keep it legal.** No illegal content, threats or fraud.
5. **Stay roughly on topic.**
6. **Build rather than burn.** Constructive criticism is welcome and wanted; wrecking things for its
   own sake is not.

Harassment means any of: repeated unwanted contact after being asked to stop, insults or threats
aimed at a person, sexual remarks or images someone did not ask for, publishing someone's private
details, or getting others to pile on. It applies whoever the target is: a contributor, a member, a
newcomer or a maintainer.

A few things are never allowed, whatever the intent or context, and skip every warning step: above
all, anything that sexually exploits a child, which is removed at once and reported to the
authorities. The full list is the Accord's
[Absolute Prohibitions](https://united-humanity.us/library#absolute-prohibitions), and what each
server does about illegal content is in the
[content policy](docs/legal/content-policy.md).

## How to report

- **On united-humanity.us**: use **Report** on the message or on the person's name, in the app or on
  the website.
- **On GitHub**: use GitHub's own "Report content" on the comment, issue or pull request, which
  reaches GitHub's staff, and tell the maintainer in the project's Discord or on united-humanity.us
  by a direct message.
- **On Discord**: message one of the server's moderators directly.
- **If someone is in danger right now**, call your local emergency number first (911 in the United
  States). Maintainers are volunteers, not police.

The person you report is not told who reported them.

## What happens

The response is proportional, in this order: a word about it, then a temporary mute or lock, then
removal from the space, then a ban. Severe harm, or repeating it after the earlier steps, skips
straight to removal; the absolute limits skip the ladder entirely. The maintainer (Shaostoul) and the
moderators he names decide, and anyone who decides must be able to say who they are and which rule
the decision rests on.

## Appeals

Any decision can be questioned. If you can still reach the space, message a maintainer or moderator
directly. If you are banned from it, open an issue at
[github.com/Shaostoul/Humanity/issues](https://github.com/Shaostoul/Humanity/issues) (it is public,
so leave private details out), or appeal on united-humanity.us if you are banned only from GitHub or
Discord. There is no deadline and no limit on asking twice. If a decision cannot be explained by a
rule above, it is wrong.

Each HumanityOS server run by someone else writes its own rules; this file binds the project's
spaces only.
