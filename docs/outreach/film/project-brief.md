## Short project description

The cinematic intro film for HumanityOS, a free, public-domain sci-fi survival game
and real-life skills platform. A united humanity reaches into a silent galaxy, colony
by colony, and stumbles into a trap no one could have seen coming.

## Context

HumanityOS is a real project, not just a film premise: an open-source, public-domain
(CC0) video game and life-tools platform built in the open since 2019 by one human
(Shaostoul) and his AI partner (Claude), with a mission of teaching real survival
skills (grow food, store water, generate power, fabricate, repair) through a
free-to-fail simulation. This film is the game's canonical opening cinematic: the
exact moment its story begins. Win or lose, this film ships as the game's actual
intro. Everything about the project is public: source code, documentation, finances,
and this film's full script.

## Narrative

Humanity, united at last, sends its First Fleet beyond the solar system knowing
nothing of what waits. The galaxy appears empty: the fleet arrives, takes root,
lights a beacon, reaches again. One system. Two. Three. At the fourth, the sky
itself turns out to be a lie: an invisible barrier catches the vanguard gently,
with inertial mercy, and the ships behind cannot stop. Sealed inside a manufactured
solar system with a lattice-enclosed star, the survivors discover Agartha: an
engineered world wrapped in shells of wreckage from a million-year war, every
species that ever came for it still in orbit, broken. There is no way out. Except
through.

Themes: competence is survival; the drills that cost nothing become the skills that
save everyone ("die in the sim, live in the world," which is literally what the game
teaches); humans and AI as one crew. The narrator is the fleet's AI, and the last
line is its confession of belonging: "We are Humanity. You included."

## Visuals

Photorealistic cinematic sci-fi, IMAX-anamorphic 21:9, physically plausible light,
near-silent vacuum realism (space does not roar here; the collisions play in hush).
Signature identity: the human fleet's hulls are golden-ratio spiral superstructures,
white ceramic and gold with teal running lights, the same Fibonacci-spiral identity
as the homesteads in the game. Palette: void black, ember gold, ceramic white, teal;
Agartha alone glows gold and jade. Mood references: the scale-awe of 2001 and
Interstellar with the physical grit of hard-SF naval fiction. Consistency is held
with locked prompt blocks, character reference sheets, three recurring faces, and
helmets everywhere else.
