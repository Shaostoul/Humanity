# Volunteers wanted

HumanityOS is free and open to everyone, and some of what it needs can only come from people. This is
the list of roles we are looking for, kept current as new ones come up. Started 8 November 2026.

Everything a volunteer gives is shared with the world the same way the project is: dedicated to the
public domain (CC0), so anyone may use it for anything, forever. Nobody is paid, and nobody is asked
for money.

**This page lists roles, never people.** The names and contact details of people who offer to help
are kept privately by the maintainer, never in this public repository.

## Roles

| Role | What it involves | What you need | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The ship computer's voice** | Reading a set of prepared lines aloud (a few hours, done in sessions), recorded at home, then agreeing in writing that the recordings and a voice made from them may be used and shared under CC0 | A quiet room and a decent microphone; any accent; we send the script | The ship's voice should be a real person who chose to give it. The free voices available today do not say whose voices they were trained on (docs/reference/findings/2026-11-08-paradee-tts.md) |
| **Voices for people aboard (NPCs)** | The same as above, for characters: shorter sets of lines, many different voices welcome, in any language | As above | Crew and neighbours who sound like different real people, in many languages |
| **Movement (motion capture)** | Recording everyday movements (walking, sitting, climbing, carrying, cooking) with whatever capture setup you have, shared under CC0 and checked the way music contributions are | A motion capture suit or a camera-based capture tool | Real movement for every body in the game (docs/design/character-animation.md) |
| **Translators** | Translating the app's words and the Library into your language, one key at a time | Fluency in a language and English | The app is for everyone, including people who do not read English |
| **Testers** | Using the app and the game and telling us what confused you, broke, or felt wrong | Any computer or phone | People new to tech are who the app is for; your first impressions are the test |

## How to offer

Send a private message to an admin in the chat at https://united-humanity.us/chat (private messages
are sealed end to end), or open an issue at https://github.com/Shaostoul/Humanity/issues (issues
there are public). Say which role and anything we should know. We aim to answer within 30 days.

## For the maintainer

When someone offers, note them privately (the app's own notes are sealed with your key), and mark a
role here as filled only in words ("one voice recorded; more welcome"), never by name unless the
person asks to be credited.
