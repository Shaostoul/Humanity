# HumanityOS for Affiliate Partners

You grow seeds, forge tools, build solar kits, write books that teach people
to do real things. Your problem is not making a good product. Your problem is
reaching the people who actually want it, without paying an ad platform to
interrupt strangers who do not.

We are building a place where those people gather. This document is an early
invitation to help shape how they find you.

## Read this first: nothing here is built yet

The partner storefront described below is planned, not built. There is no
sign-up form, no rate card, and no integration to install today. If you read
this hoping to buy placement this quarter, we will waste your time, and we
would rather say so in the second paragraph than the last.

What is real today: HumanityOS has live encrypted chat, task planning, maps,
marketplace listings, a library, and an identity system you can try at
https://united-humanity.us. The game world, where the storefronts would
live, is in active development and not fully playable yet. We build in
public, and some of what works is still rough.

So this is not a sales sheet. It is a request: if the idea below sounds like
something you would want to be part of, talk to us while it is still soft
enough to shape.

## The vision, honestly in future tense

HumanityOS is building a simulation, still in development, where people
will practice real skills: growing food, managing water, budgeting power,
building shelter. The plan is a shopping district inside that world. Your real store would appear
there as a kiosk or storefront a player can walk into. Browsing the shelves
would open your real catalog. Buying would happen on your site, on your
terms, with your checkout.

The moment matters more than the storefront. A player who just finished
planning a real garden bed, in a tool that helped them lay it out, needs
seeds. Right then, in that state of mind, they could walk into your seed
shop. Not an ad wedged into their scrolling. A store, visited on purpose, at
the exact moment your product is the answer to their actual question.

That is the whole idea. We think it is a better way to meet a customer than
anything an attention market sells.

## Why this fits our mission, and where the line is

HumanityOS exists to end dependence, not to manufacture wants. Our mission
is that every family can meet its own needs: food, water, power, shelter,
knowledge. That mission decides who fits here.

Partners whose goods build capability fit naturally: seeds, hand tools,
solar and water equipment, repair parts, workshop supplies, books and plans
that teach. If your product leaves the buyer more able to provide for
themselves, we are pointed the same direction.

Attention-farming, junk that exists to be replaced, and anything designed to
create the dependence we are trying to end do not fit, and no check changes
that. We will say no to money that fights the mission. We would rather grow
slowly with partners we respect than quickly with ones we have to apologize
for.

## Who you would be reaching

Small numbers today, and we will not pretend otherwise. But an unusual kind
of person: someone who chose to spend their free time learning to grow food,
purify water, and fix what breaks. They arrive at a catalog with a plan in
hand and intent behind it. They are the opposite of a passive scroller, and
they talk to each other.

## What partnership will never include

Some things are settled now, before any code is written, because they are
the point:

- No user data for sale. Not aggregated, not anonymized, not ever.
- No tracking pixels or third-party surveillance inside HumanityOS.
- No pay-for-placement that deceives players. If something is a partner
  storefront, it will be labeled as one. A recommendation will never be
  secretly an ad.

One of the five adversaries this project exists to end is fraud: profit
through deception. A marketplace that tricks its own people dies of the
disease it was built to cure. You can read how we define that line at
https://united-humanity.us/library#the-five-adversaries. Hold us to it.

## The money, plainly

For you, a partnership would most likely mean ordinary affiliate or
referral arrangements on your existing terms, transparent to players and
simple for you. Those details are exactly what early partners get to help
decide; there is no rate card yet because there is no product yet.

The backdrop that explains why there is no rate card: HumanityOS is
public domain (CC0), with no company, no nonprofit, no equity, and
nothing for sale. The project is one maintainer and volunteers, funded by
personal gifts. Any partner storefront will follow that same honesty.

## What to do now

The concept is in design. Early partners will shape how storefronts look,
how catalogs connect, how referrals work, and what the rules of the
district are. That influence is the honest offer at this stage, because it
is the only thing that exists yet.
