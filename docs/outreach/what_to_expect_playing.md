# What to Expect: Playing HumanityOS

You have seen plenty of games promise you a new life in space. This page
is not a trailer. HumanityOS is in active development and not fully
playable yet, so what follows is an honest picture of the game we are
building: what an ordinary day will feel like, what the big moments look
like, and exactly what already works today.

If you read to the end and think "I want to be there when it opens up,"
we would love to have you.

## Where you live

Your home is a homestead aboard a great ship. Not a barracks, not a
respawn point: a place that is yours. A garden that feeds you, water and
power you manage yourself, a kitchen, a workshop, and room to grow.

Nobody hands you rations and nobody sends you a bill. The ship gives you
ground, light, and water. What you make of them is up to you. Your days
are your own: tend the garden, cook what you harvested, craft and repair
what you need, and learn whatever pulls at you next.

## The shape of a day

Here is the rhythm we are building toward.

Morning rounds start in the greenhouse. See what ripened overnight,
check the water, harvest what is ready, start seeds in the empty trays.
Then a glance at power and stores. A homestead runs on loops, and
mornings are when you close them.

Midday might be a quest. Quests here are lessons wearing a story: get
water clean, keep a stubborn crop alive through a hard stretch, build
the thing you have been doing without. Each one teaches the way it would
really be done, because that is the point of the whole game. What your
character learns, you learn. The garden in the game works the way a
garden works.

Afternoon, trade. Your greenhouse made more than you can eat, and a
neighbor grew something you did not. Surplus moving between neighbors is
how a street becomes a community, in the game and outside it.

Evenings are for the slow projects: a repair you have been putting off,
a new corner of the homestead taking shape, a skill practiced until it
sticks. Then the lights down the corridor go dim, and tomorrow the
greenhouse will need you again.

No timers pushing you, no daily chores designed by someone else. The
quiet loop is the game.

## Playing together

You will have neighbors. Real people, keeping their own homesteads,
learning the same skills at their own pace. You trade with them, build
alongside them, and ask them how they got their peppers to do that.

Chat is woven through all of it, and it already works today. The same
chat on the website, with end-to-end encrypted private messages, runs
inside the app, so the conversation does not stop when the game does.

## Quiet days and big moments

Daily play is the quiet loop, and it is meant to be. But some days will
not be quiet.

Special events are server-wide moments hosted on the official server:
things that happen to everyone at once, that the whole community lives
through together. Not a patch note you read afterward. A day you were
there for, alongside everyone else who was.

## The opening chapter

When HumanityOS officially releases, the main server will host the
opening chapter of the official story, played live by everyone who shows
up for it.

The premise is public, and it is all we will spoil: the fleet arrives in
a solar system and finds it hidden inside a cloaked Dyson swarm, a vast
cloud of built structures wrapped around the star, concealing everything
within.

Who built it, why it is hidden, and what happens next is not written in
a cutscene. It is played, not read. The people there that day will be
the ones who find out.

## What is real today, honestly

Honesty is a house rule here, so plainly:

Real and working now:

- The world. A 3D world you can move through, in active development and
  changing fast.
- The homestead systems, in early form: gardening, cooking, crafting,
  mining and refining, building, skills, and quests all exist and run.
- Chat, tasks, maps, marketplace listings, and the library are live
  today at https://united-humanity.us and inside the app.
- Your identity: a key you own on your own device. No account, no
  password, nothing a company can take away.

Still being built:

- The connected daily rhythm described above. The pieces exist; the day
  that flows through them is what we are assembling now.
- Playing together inside the world itself.
- Special events and the official story.

If you download the app today you will find a world mid-construction:
some of it working, some of it rough, some of it broken. That is what
early looks like, and we build in the open on purpose. Every bug you
find and tell us about makes the game better.
