# How HumanityOS Helps

Most of what keeps you alive arrives through systems you do not control.
Food comes from a supply chain you cannot see. Water and power come from
providers who set the price. Knowledge and community increasingly come
through platforms built to hold your attention and sell it, not to make
you more capable. If any of those links break, or simply decide you can
no longer afford them, you have very little to fall back on.

That is the problem HumanityOS exists to shrink: needs gated behind
permission, supply chains with no layer beneath them, and tools that
profit from your dependence instead of ending it.

## What HumanityOS is

HumanityOS is one free app. It has two halves that share the same
skills.

One half is practical tools for real life, live today: chat with
end-to-end encrypted private messages and no account or signup, a task
planner, maps that zoom from the whole galaxy to your own street, a
marketplace for trading directly with others, a library of the
project's knowledge, and an identity that lives on your device as a
key, not on a company's server. There are no ads and nothing is
harvested. The app and your data live on your device and work without
a connection; the parts that talk to other people sync when you are
back online.

The other half is a game world we are building that teaches real
self-sufficiency: growing food, collecting and cleaning water,
generating power, and repairing what breaks. You practice in a
simulation where a mistake costs nothing, and the same knowledge carries
into your actual garden, your actual rain barrel, your actual power
budget. The game world is in active development and is not fully
playable yet.

Everything, the code, the data, the documents, is released into the
public domain. No company owns it. No one ever will.

## What help looks like

**For a person.** Help means a shorter list of things you must ask
permission for. Someone who can grow part of their own food, catch some
of their own water, and plan their way off one recurring bill is not
rich, but they are harder to price out of their own life. HumanityOS
aims to teach those skills for free and give you the planning tools to
actually follow through.

**For a neighborhood.** Help means neighbors who can find each other.
The maps show the place; the chat and the member list show the people.
The marketplace lets surplus move
directly between households with no middleman taking a cut. The chat
lets people organize without a platform in the middle deciding who may
speak. A street where five households can each hold their own weight for
a season is a street that handles a bad month together instead of
failing one by one.

**For a nation.** Help means a stronger layer under everything else. A
country of capable households does not fail all at once when a supply
chain is interrupted, and it recovers faster when disaster hits, because
shocks stay local and brief. This costs a treasury nothing: the tools
are free, the knowledge is free, and none of it is owned by any foreign
company or any company at all. Nothing here competes with a government.
It quietly reduces what governments must handle in a crisis.

## What it costs

Nothing. Not as a promotion, but structurally.

HumanityOS is public domain under CC0. That is a legal act, not a
pricing decision: no one holds a copyright to sell, license, or fence
off later. There is no company behind it, no nonprofit, no investors, no
equity, and nothing for sale. There is no premium tier waiting behind
the free one, because there is no owner to collect on it. A promise of
"free forever" depends on someone keeping it. Public domain does not.
It is free the way arithmetic is free.

## Where the project is today

Early, and built in the open on purpose. One maintainer working in
public, with AI collaborators and a small circle of community testers.
The practical tools listed above are real and live, and the network
around them is still young and quiet. The game world is under heavy
construction. Some of what you touch will be rough, and some will break.
We would rather show you the real state of it than a polished promise.
