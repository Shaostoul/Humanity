# Update: 26 October to 11 November 2026

Posts for X (a thread), Discord and Facebook, covering everything since the update of 25 October
(`2026-10-25-update.md`): v0.1548.0 to v0.1556.0, 10 releases on GitHub (counted with
`gh release list` on 2026-11-11). Every claim comes from the release notes and the list below
(`docs/outreach/since-last-update.md`, moved here). Two things are merged but not yet released when
this was written, and the posts word them as coming in the next update: the one music switch that
plays from the app's start all session, and Ministral 3 3B in Settings > AI. Talking characters are
NOT built yet and are not claimed. Words: "recovery phrase" and "no sign-up" (CLAUDE.md, account
words). The AI-is-optional rule had its own post on 9 November (`2026-11-09-ai-optional.md`); here it
is one item among the rest.

## Discord

**HumanityOS: 26 October to 11 November** 🧍

10 releases since the last update. The theme was **people**: real bodies in the game, and an app that treats you like one.

**Real people in the game** 🧬
- **Real human bodies** for you, other players and the crew, made from MakeHuman's free (CC0) body data by our own converter, and moving with Quaternius's free (CC0) animation clips: idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump and sit.
- **Your own look:** 248 face and body sliders in 21 groups (nose, eyes, mouth, cheeks, jaw, ears, brows, torso, arms and legs and more), with three ancestry blends as quick starting points.
- **Real clothes and hair:** 14 outfits, 6 pairs of shoes, 2 hats, 10 hair styles, 12 eyebrows and 4 eyelashes, each fitted to your shape so skin never pokes through. Shoes lift you by their sole. Other players see your look when you press Done.
- **Feet on the world:** feet stand on floors, stair treads, ramps and the slopes of the map, and a planted foot stays put.
- **Things to do with a body:** crouch (hold or toggle), jump, sit on chairs and couches (your legs hang from a seat that's too high for you), lie in a bed, climb a ladder through its hatch with your hands on the rungs, work at a bench, and open a hinged door with your hand as you walk up to it.
- **True first person:** look down and see your own legs, clothes and hands. Your shadow keeps its head.
- **Choose your pace:** Realistic walking at 1.4 m/s, Brisk at 2, or the quicker Simplified pace.

**Light and shadow** 🌗
- **Shadows** from the room lamps that matter most where you're looking, and from the sun near you: people, furniture and the home all cast them now. Before this, nothing near you cast a shadow at all.
- **Every graphics setting says what it actually changes** and what it costs on your computer: measured live, worked out from your choice, a download size, or "nothing measurable" with the reason.
- **40 detailed models** from NVIDIA's free SimReady library in the homestead and the Commons: a kitchen counter with its basin and tap, a workbench, a vise, a tool chest, hand tools and more.
- **Clothing textures** at 512, 1K, or an optional 2K download, each with its video memory shown before you pick. No stutter when a crowd arrives.

**Music and sound** 🎶
- **A live score** at A = 432 Hz: deep drums, steel pan, strings and bells, rising with danger and settling back. Settings > Music.
- **The ship hums**, tuned to the music, louder near the drive.
- **Free recorded instruments** to install from Settings > Audio > Instruments.
- **Every sound has its own level**, and there's a Notifications volume, so turning clicks down never silences chimes.
- **Coming in the next update:** one switch, Music on the app's pages, on by default, playing from the moment the app opens.

**Privacy you can see** 🔒
- **Hidden means hidden:** turning off "Show others when I am online" keeps you out of every list and announcement.
- **Erasing your account takes everything that's yours**, and both erase windows say exactly what stays.
- **Bug reports no longer show who sent them**, except to admins and moderators.
- **A direct message and your own copy can no longer be matched up on the server.**
- **Files in members-only rooms** open only for that room's members.
- **A privacy policy** that says what the server keeps, for how long and who can see it: united-humanity.us/privacy
- The website no longer asks GitHub on every page.

**AI, only if you want it** 🔌
- **Settings > AI** can download a small AI model, IBM's Granite 4.0 1B (about 1 GB), and the engine that runs it (llama.cpp). Its size, maker and licence show before anything downloads; a stopped download picks up where it left off; every file is checked; Remove deletes it all again.
- **Coming in the next update:** Ministral 3 3B by Mistral AI, a stronger model beside it.
- Nothing AI is installed unless you choose it, and everything in HumanityOS works without it. Characters who talk back are planned, not built yet.

**The real world on the map** 🗺️
- **Silverdale and Seattle** come from map packs we build ourselves from OpenStreetMap, GEDTM30 bare-earth heights and ESA WorldCover. The Silverdale waterfront slopes into the water like the real shore.
- **Roads sit in beds** cut into the ground and buildings stand on level pads, with full detail near you.

**The app** 🧭
- **A double top menu:** eight categories, each with its row of pages.
- **Back and Forward** like a web browser: the arrows, your mouse's side buttons, Alt+Left and Alt+Right.
- **Text size from 100% to 200%** across the whole app.
- **Desktop notifications** with quiet hours, people you always let through, and a tray icon.
- **Chat channels** each say who can read and who can post; anyone can read the public ones without signing up. #general is now #global, and #feedback is on every server.
- **Play is the game's hub:** your world and Enter the world, plus a card for Characters, Quests, Inventory and Crafting. Holding Play no longer drops you into the game.
- **Gameplay settings you can turn off:** body heat, illness and injury, farm animals, carrying weight and even death, each with gentler and realistic levels too, and each saying what it changes.
- **A tidier Inventory**, in coloured sections.

Credits for MakeHuman, Quaternius, NVIDIA SimReady, OpenStreetMap contributors, GEDTM30, ESA WorldCover, llama.cpp and IBM Granite are in the app's Credits page.

Free and open source, always: <https://united-humanity.us> · <https://github.com/Shaostoul/Humanity>

## What shipped (moved from `since-last-update.md`)

### v0.1556.0 (11 November 2026)

- People, furniture and the home cast shadows from the room lamps and the sun; before, nothing near you did.
- Play is the game's hub: your world with Enter the world, and a card for each game page. Play no longer starts the game when held.
- Gameplay settings can be turned Off (body heat, illness, carrying weight, death and more), and each choice says what it changes.
- The music starts when HumanityOS opens, and the Inventory is tighter, in coloured sections.

### v0.1555.0 (11 November 2026)

- Shape your own face and body with 248 sliders (nose, eyes, jaw, ears and more), and wear real clothes, shoes, hats and hair fitted to your shape; others see your look.
- Sit on chairs and beds, lie down, climb ladders through their hatches, work at a bench, and open doors with your hand.
- True first person: look down and see your own legs, clothes and hands.
- Every graphics setting says what it changes and what it costs on your computer; clothing textures at 512, 1K or an optional 2K download.
- No stutter when crowds arrive, clothes in under a third of the video memory, and downloads about 150 MB smaller.

### v0.1554.1 and v0.1554.0 (10 November 2026)

- Shape your body on the character page with build, proportions and ancestry sliders; other players see the shape you made.
- Feet stand on what is under them: ship floors, stair treads, ramps and the slopes of the map, and a planted foot stays put.
- Hold C to crouch (others see it), and jumping plays a take-off, a moment in the air and a landing.
- Settings > Gameplay > Animation: Realistic, or Simplified for older computers. The character page shows your height in metres.
- AI only if you want it: Settings > AI can download a small model, IBM's Granite 4.0 1B (about 1 GB), showing its size, maker and licence first; downloads resume, every file is checked, and Remove deletes it all. Everything works without it.
- Map roads sit in beds cut into the ground and buildings stand on level pads, with full detail near you.
- Your own body on a planet now stands where you are, not 400 km up with the station.
- v0.1554.1: the same update for macOS and Linux, whose builds had stopped on one line meant only for Windows.

### v0.1553.0 (9 November 2026)

- Real people: you, other players and the crew are now real human bodies, a woman and a man made from MakeHuman's free body data, instead of the plain test figure.
- They idle, walk, jog and sprint with animation clips from Quaternius's free animation library, fitted to each body so the feet stay planted.
- Choose your body on the character page; other players see the one you picked, and your height reshapes the body itself.
- Settings > Gameplay > Walking pace: Simplified (the quick pace) or Realistic (a 1.4 m/s walk and a 4 m/s run, the pace of a real person).
- Silverdale and Seattle come from the new map packs: the waterfront slopes gently into the water like the real shore, with no sheer cliffs and no dry harbour.
- The website's download page now gets each release by the server fetching it from GitHub and checking every file, instead of an upload that could hang.

### v0.1552.0 (9 November 2026)

- People have bodies: you in third person, other players and the crew stand, turn, walk and run, and their legs keep pace with how fast they move.
- Third person walks your body through the ship with the camera over your shoulder.
- 40 detailed models from NVIDIA's free SimReady library in the homestead and the Commons: a kitchen counter with its basin and tap, a workbench, a vise, a tool chest, hand tools, a fire extinguisher, a first aid kit and more.
- A Medical Bay set to install from Settings > Graphics > Furniture models.
- Settings > Graphics > Furniture detail, which shows the video memory each choice uses and says plainly when your graphics card is short.
- Furniture that was drawn from its corner now stands where it belongs, and entering the world is faster.
- The OpenStreetMap credit shows wherever map data is drawn, and heights are credited to their real sources.
