## Discord

### Message 1

**HumanityOS update: 13 to 16 November 2026**
Seven releases this week (v0.1558.0 to v0.1564.0). HumanityOS is a free, open-source project with one goal: end poverty and bring people together. This week the people in the game started talking, the homes got finished rooms, and the app gained skills, organisations and a chat that follows you around.

**Talking characters**
Walk up to a crew member and press E to talk about the ship, their work, the cause or the way. You can take on work, trade, or ask them to mark the way. It all works with written lines. If you choose to install an AI model, a "Say anything" box lets you ask in your own words, and every AI line is tagged. Nothing AI is installed unless you choose it.

**Skills**
A base set of skills with no level cap. Each stage says in plain words what you can do ("I can..."), and everything you do in the game trains its own skill. Every statement was checked against primary sources before shipping, and 103 were corrected. Practice now feels like practice: repeating the same task gives less until you rest a day, a first try counts extra, and reading the Library guide for your stage or practising beside someone more skilled helps. The Skills page says why each practice gave what it gave, and Settings sets how strict it is (Off, Simplified or Realistic).

**Organisations**
Members, roles, invitations, a directory and a record of changes, kept on members' own devices: the server never sees who belongs or what changed. Changes reach every member as sealed messages, and an owner can hand an organisation on. Guilds are now part of Organisations. The first real group on it is Sponsor-a-Can.

### Message 2

**Homes**
- Row Street: four row house layouts (two bedrooms for four, three for five, three couples, five friends) on their own street with one open green behind the yards. Neighbours' houses are lit inside day and night, with gardens along the yards.
- Rooms look finished: skirting and crown moulding with mitred corners, casings round every door and window, sills, and chair and picture rails. Choose them per room or per wall in the build editor.
- Furniture is the size it looks and stands flush against the walls. Nothing blocks a doorway any more, every room can be walked into round its furniture, and you stop when you walk into furniture.
- Glass roofs carry glazing bars, so they read as roofs.
- The mushroom racks wear fitted frosted covers with a zip front that rolls up while you tend them.

**Graphics**
- Smoother edges: temporal anti-aliasing is on by default, so hair, chairs and the horizon lose their jagged steps.
- Far forests draw out to the full tree distance, and cost less: each distant tree card is a tight outline around its tree, and the graphics card draws only the ones in view.
- Shadows from room lights cost 20 to 43 percent less, and set to Off they cost nothing. Frosted plastic and glass cast faint tinted shadows and blur what is behind them.
- Crowds walk through doorways, round tables and round each other.

### Message 3

**Music**
- Endless music: a new piece every few minutes, each with its own key, song shape, chords and melodies. Switching genre is heard in seconds.
- New genres: electro swing, deep dubstep, house, drum and bass, synthwave and ambient, with new synths and 808 and 909 style drum kits.
- The Music page: explore pieces by seed and genre, tune one, keep favourites, export them as WAV files, and watch a piano roll as it plays.
- Eleven free instrument libraries, plus optional packs (417 instruments in seven full packs) to download from Settings. Long recordings play from disk, so the full Steinway takes about 15 MB of memory instead of 452 MB and sounds the same.
- One place chooses what plays: the Music page's choice keeps playing after you leave it.

**The app**
- A chat footer on every page except Chat: one line folded, or the latest messages and a box to type in unfolded, with the same conversation and draft as the Chat page (Alt+C, or turn it off in Settings).
- A model inspector: see a 3D model's attachment points, axes, size and collision shapes before you use it, in the app and on the website.
- A Pulleys mode on the Calculator designs a block and tackle for any load and any puller, and says what it costs in rope and time.
- Garden pests, pollination, soil pH and picking each have Off, Gentle and Realistic.
- A first Android build: the app now builds as a phone app on GitHub's free build machines, and says plainly what a phone can't do yet.

### Message 4

**A new licence**
The code is now under the GNU AGPL 3.0, so anyone who runs a changed HumanityOS server must share its source. The Library and docs are under CC BY-SA 4.0, so Wikipedia's text can come in, credited. Game data and the Humanity Accord stay public domain, and a short policy keeps the name HumanityOS for the real thing.

**Under the hood**
- The simulation runs without a screen: a week of the homestead in about half a second, with a person kept alive by a daily routine, and a walk through every floor of the homestead in about five seconds with no window, matching the game's own check leg for leg.
- Fixed: a new player's privacy question could be buried under garden notices that also blocked its button; the chat's text box could hide below the window; overlays blocked clicks under them; videos could loop without a picture on a busy computer.

**Plans written this week**
Every activity in the game catalogued, the everyday services we still lack next to Microsoft's, what it takes to run on phones and every other device, a better radiation shield, and a room-by-room program for homes.

Also out since the last post: the music plays from the moment HumanityOS opens, and Settings > AI offers Ministral 3 3B beside Granite (optional, like all AI here).
