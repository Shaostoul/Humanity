## Discord

**HumanityOS: the week of 21 to 27 September** 🚀

Nearly 100 releases this week. The theme was **the ship keeps you alive**, and the garden, the body and the weather all got real science under them.

**Life aboard the ship**
- Closed loops for air and water: plants breathe out water and the air handlers return it to the tanks; CO2 from people and mushroom tents feeds the crops.
- The ship's reactor powers every home in the default mode, and every watt-hour you draw is metered and saved. A buildable solar panel cuts your draw watt for watt. Realistic mode runs your home on only what you generate and store.
- Batteries now carry the load through the night.
- Stored things survive a restart: battery charge, tank water, fuel drums, the pantry, freezer and silo.

**Your body**
- A real heat balance (the Gagge two-node model used in ASHRAE's thermal comfort work): what you wear, the wind, rain, sweat and shivering all count. **Forgiving** (default) and **Realistic** modes.
- Rest is a short nap now; a night in a bed restores your energy.

**Building**
- Pieces go in your hands with a see-through ghost; R turns them, E places them. Roofs sit on their walls.
- Build on a planet's ground as well as aboard. A roof over three walls keeps the wind and rain off you.
- Built beds sleep the night; built chests keep what you store.

**Garden and crafting**
- Real N-P-K, soil pH with lime and sulfur, pollination, pests managed the way university extension guides teach, weeds, greenhouse humidity and fungal disease, harvests at real plant spacing, and mushrooms that fruit in their tents.
- Crafting needs its hand tools, tools wear out, stations need power, byproducts come out at real ratios, soap needs lye.

**Time and weather**
- One clock: a 24-hour day by default, configurable, where an hour is an hour and a single time-speed setting speeds everything up.
- Temperature, pressure and wind by latitude, season and altitude, fitted to 30 years of NCEP/NCAR reanalysis. Rain or snow is decided by the air where it falls.

**The view**
- Aurora curtains, clouds that form fronts and arcs and read white from orbit, the ocean glint's stripes and the sea's colour blocks fixed, and light that bounces around the ship's rooms.

**Library** 📚
- 8 new guides: Growing Mushrooms, Testing and Correcting Soil, Watering and Irrigation, Extending the Season, Heating Water, Perennials Fruit and Nuts, Batteries and Storage, Why Seasons Happen. Plus fire performance research on staff tubes, fuels and clothing.
- Each of these got an independent second pass that checked every claim against its source. Those passes caught real mistakes this week, which is why we do them.

We also removed an old rule that made Wi-Fi routers harm crops, because the evidence doesn't support it.

Free and open source: https://united-humanity.us · https://github.com/Shaostoul/Humanity

## Facebook

A week of HumanityOS 🌱

HumanityOS is a free, open-source project with a big goal: end poverty and bring people together. Part of it is a game that teaches real survival skills, like growing food, keeping water clean, staying warm and building shelter, by simulating them honestly.

This week we shipped nearly 100 updates. Here are the highlights.

🚀 Life aboard the ship. The air and water now work in loops, the way a real spacecraft's would. Plants breathe out water that the air handlers collect and send back to the tanks, and the carbon dioxide people breathe out feeds the crops. The ship's reactor powers every home, and every bit of power you use is metered, so you can see what your household really needs. Build solar panels to draw less.

🔥 Staying warm. Your body now keeps itself warm the way a real body does. Clothing, wind, rain and sweat all matter. Build a roof over three walls and the wind and rain stop reaching you. There's a forgiving mode for relaxed play and a realistic mode for the full challenge.

🏡 Building. You now see a see-through outline of a wall or roof before you place it, you can turn it, and roofs sit on top of their walls. You can build on a planet's surface too. Beds let you sleep through the night, and chests keep your things safe.

🥬 The garden. Crops need the same things real crops need: nutrients, the right soil pH, pollination, water and light. Pests are handled the way university garden guides teach. Mushrooms grow in their own humid tents.

🌦️ Time and weather. A day is 24 hours, an hour is an hour, and you can speed time up if you like. The weather now depends on where you stand: it's colder up a mountain, the seasons are opposite in the two hemispheres, and whether it rains or snows depends on the air around you.

📚 The Library. Eight new guides you can read for free in the app, on growing mushrooms, testing your soil, watering, growing earlier and later in the year, heating water safely, fruit trees and berries, home batteries, and why we have seasons. Each one was checked a second time against its sources before it went in.

One more thing: we removed an old rule that made Wi-Fi routers harm crops, because the evidence doesn't support it. We'd rather teach what's true.

Everything is free and open source. 👉 https://united-humanity.us
