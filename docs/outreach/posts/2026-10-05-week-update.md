## Discord

**HumanityOS: the week of 29 September to 5 October** 🛠️

78 releases since the last update. The theme was **playing together**: every player gets a home of their own on one mothership, and you can meet, trade and build side by side.

**Playing together** 🤝
- **Your own home on the ship:** when you join a server, your home gets its own plot and you arrive at your own front door. Twelve plots line First Street, over a kilometre long; the thirteenth player arrives as a guest and is told so.
- **Meet in the Commons:** walk out of your home, down your corridor, into the ship's Commons, and see the other players there. Players appear within 250 m and drop out past 300 m, so you only get who you could see.
- **Build together:** on your own plot you can build foundations, walls, windows and roofs, and everyone nearby sees them go up. Nobody else can build on your plot, the server keeps every piece, and nobody is told who built what.
- **Players look like themselves** (skin tone, hair, height) and walk smoothly.
- **One clock:** everyone in a shared world sees the same sun, and a day is a real day.
- **Trades hand over real items**, worn tools still worn.
- **The crew live aboard:** they work in the Commons and eat in the mess hall from the same stores players do, and turn to face where they walk.
- **The fleet ledger:** the fleet's stores are unlimited for now, and Inventory > The fleet shows whether you've given more than you've used. Only you see your own.

**A real game from the first minute** 🎮
- Normal mode with your progress kept is the default: things get used up, tools wear, nothing is free.
- The first ten minutes walk you through it: check your vitals, eat, plant, make a tool, bring in iron and smelt it, build a chest, step out of your front door.
- A Death setting: Simplified loses nothing; Realistic leaves your backpack where you fell, for an hour of play.
- Your save keeps its last ten versions, and Settings > Data can put any of them back.

**Survival, more honest** 🔥
- Sweating costs water, and humid heat costs more.
- What you can carry follows gravity (about 132 kg on Mars), and in Realistic mode too much slows you down.
- **Campfire** builds a real stone-ring fire from 6 stones and 3 logs: it burns its logs, warms you only in a clear line (never through a wall, never under a roof), and you add logs with E.
- The **space heater** warms the air of the room it stands in.
- **Food poisoning** drains your body's water over a day or two and passes; drinking keeps you well, and oral rehydration salts work best.
- **Medicine** works: bandages and medkits heal and stop bleeding, antibiotics clear bacterial infections only.
- Hunger and thirst now keep real time at any frame rate (a full stomach used to stay full forever at 60 frames a second).

**The free Library** 📚
- 75 new guides this week; 134 of the 143 curriculum topics now have one. Among them: Water-Bath and Pressure Canning, First Aid Until Help Arrives (CPR, choking, bleeding), Heating a Home Safely, Fuels and Their Hazards, Choosing and Running a Generator, Mental Health Under Strain, Toilets and Human Waste, The Germs That Matter, When Food or Water Makes You Sick, and guides on building, soil, animals, electricity and finding your way.
- Each was checked against its sources before it went in, and the health and safety guides by a second, independent reviewer.

**Fairer, and more real** ⚖️
- No more free money at the trading post: buying parts, crafting and selling back can't make money at any craft grade, and a test checks every recipe.
- Vehicles are built from about their own weight in real materials (a motorcycle 2,500 credits, a sedan 14,500).
- The trading post sells what it lists (140 of its 300 goods couldn't be bought before).
- The generator burns its own fuel, not paint.

**The world** 🌍
- The stars are where they really are for your place and date, and fade at dawn.
- Trees stand on the ground in Silverdale (they floated about 37 m up).
- Greenhouse plants show their real colours, round things are no longer inside out, and the ship's districts look like what they are.
- Pipes are marked the way ships' pipes are (ISO 14726).

**Fixes you'll notice** 🔧
- The Windows download is the whole game (it used to look like grey boxes).
- Chat works on older phones (reported by a user in Nigeria).
- An erased account stays erased on every device.
- The game can film itself: smooth landscape and portrait clips, made in the game.

Free and open source: https://united-humanity.us · https://github.com/Shaostoul/Humanity

## LinkedIn

A week of building HumanityOS in public: 78 releases, and two milestones I'm proud of.

For anyone new: HumanityOS is a free, open-source, public-domain project with one goal, to help people provide for themselves and each other so that fewer people live in poverty. There's nothing to buy and no sign-up.

1. A free practical library that grew by 75 guides in one week. Step-by-step guides for people starting from zero: canning food safely, CPR for adults, children and babies, heating a home without carbon monoxide risk, running a generator, keeping water and food safe when the power is out, and supporting someone through a mental health crisis. The desktop app is a survival simulation where what you learn should hold up in real life, and this week it became multiplayer in a real sense: every player has their own home on one mothership, can walk out to meet the others, trade real items, and build on their own plot while their neighbours watch. The server checks who may build where, and nobody is told who built what.

It's still one person building full time, with AI doing a lot of the typing, and every change is public.
