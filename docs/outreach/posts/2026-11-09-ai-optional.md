## Discord

**AI in HumanityOS: optional, never required** 🔌

Some features we're planning can use AI: characters in the game who talk back, a helper that answers questions about the app, reading text aloud, and reading a food label with your camera.

Here's the rule we've set for all of it:

- **Nothing AI comes with the app.** Each AI part is a separate download that you choose in Settings.
- **The engine that runs AI is a separate download too.** If you never choose it, there is no AI on your machine at all.
- **Everything works without it.** Characters fall back to written lines. The label checker takes typed text or a barcode.
- **When you do choose it, it runs on your own computer.** It's labelled "AI" or "computer voice" wherever it appears.

Today HumanityOS has no AI in it. This rule makes sure it stays your choice.

Free, open source, public domain: https://united-humanity.us
