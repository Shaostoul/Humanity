I am Michael Boisson (Shaostoul), the maintainer of HumanityOS. I am

WHAT IT IS

HumanityOS is one free app that teaches real self-sufficiency (growing
food, collecting water, generating power) through a game world and
real-life tools that share the same skills, plus the everyday
infrastructure a community needs: chat with post-quantum end-to-end
encrypted private messages, task planning, maps, a person-to-person
marketplace, and a library of the project's founding documents. It has
been my full-time work for seven years (started as Project Universe in
2019), with roughly 1,100 public releases, built as one human pair
programming with AI in the open.

Live: https://united-humanity.us
Code: https://github.com/Shaostoul/Humanity

WHY IT FITS FUTO

- No accounts. Identity is a cryptographic key on the user's own
  device, backed up by a seed phrase. There is nothing a company can
  suspend, and no company to do it.
- No surveillance. No ads, no telemetry, nothing harvested. Private
  messages are sealed on-device; the server holds ciphertext.
- Self-hostable and federated. Anyone can run their own server in
  minutes; identities move freely between servers. No single point of
  failure or control.
- Public domain (CC0), not just open source. Nobody, including me, can
  ever fence it off, relicense it, or sell it back to the people it
  was built for. Capture is structurally impossible.
- It runs on a $20 a month server, and works offline, because the
  people who need it most have the least.

THE MONEY, PLAINLY

I publish the project's actual books, itemized, on the site:
https://united-humanity.us/library#for-funders-and-sponsors

The short version: income is about $620 a month (a $600 nonprofit
stipend and about $21 from Patreon), costs are about $241 a month (the
AI development subscription and hosting), and I live on the roughly
$380 that remains. I can do that because my family houses me and I
live frugally. A microgrant would be months of runway: it keeps me
building full time, fixes the car that gets me anywhere, and replaces
things I have put off replacing for years.

WHAT YOU WOULD BE FUNDING

Not a company (there is none) and not a promise. The release history
is public and averages multiple shipped releases per day. The grant
funds the continuation of exactly what is already visibly happening,
by the person doing it.

Thank you for reading, and for funding the kind of software you fund.
