Notes: stiffness = how much it flexes (higher = stiffer). Bending strength = load before it takes a permanent bend (yield). Dent index = yield strength x wall squared, a rough ring-crush scaling for denting on a drop, useful only for comparing, not a prediction. Heat carried = thermal conductivity x metal cross-section (how much heat the tube walls conduct toward the hands).

Strength basis: "tube min" = a published specification minimum for tube in that alloy and condition. "sheet min", "strip min", "bar min" and "bar/plate min" = the specification minimum for another product form, used because no tube minimum was found. "typical" = a typical value, not a guaranteed minimum, so it reads high next to the minimum-based rows.

Row notes:

[1] Aluminium 6063-T832: The Kaiser drawn-tube minimum depends on wall: 248 MPa for 0.025-0.049 in, 241 MPa for 0.050-0.250 in. Each table uses the band for its own wall.

[2] Stainless 201 annealed: No tube minimum exists for 201; this is the ASTM A240 sheet minimum for type 201-2. Tube is welded from strip, and the 201-1 variant has a lower minimum. With the A240 type 201-1 minimum: bending strength 0.74 at 0.040 in and 1.08 at 0.065 in (dent index 0.41 at 0.040 in).

[3] Carbon steel 1020 DOM (as drawn): Retail DOM is sold as "1020/1026" and is often supplied stress-relieved. The row assumes as-drawn 1020 (A513 minimum 414 MPa). With stress-relieved 1020 (A513 minimum 379 MPa): bending strength 1.07 at 0.040 in and 1.57 at 0.065 in (dent index 0.60 at 0.040 in). With as-drawn 1026 (A513 minimum 483 MPa): bending strength 1.37 at 0.040 in and 2.00 at 0.065 in (dent index 0.76 at 0.040 in).

[4] Magnesium AZ31B tube: Bending yields the compression face first, and AZ31B tube is much weaker in compression: BTI lists 83 MPa (12 ksi) compressive yield against 165 MPa tensile, both typical. The row uses the ASTM B107 tensile minimum, so it overstates the load before a permanent bend by about 1.3x. The figures that follow mix a typical compressive value with the minimum-based rows; no compressive minimum was found. With the ~83 MPa typical compressive yield instead: bending strength 0.23 at 0.040 in and 0.34 at 0.065 in (dent index 0.13 at 0.040 in).
