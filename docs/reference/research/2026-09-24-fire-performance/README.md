# Fire performance research, 2026-09-24

Raw research gathered on **2026-09-24** for the Fire Disciples Facebook posts and the Library guides on fire staffs, staff tubes, fire performance fuels and clothing for fire performance. It is kept here because it is the evidence base for those guides and for the planned game data (docs/design/fire-props-and-combustion.md, rung F1: one source of truth for the Library and the simulation).

**Read this before using it.** These files are research notes, not adjudicated facts. Each finding carries its source URL and a short quotation, but individual findings were later corrected: first by a datasheet audit (alloy-corrections.json), then by adversarial fact-checkers while the posts and guides were written. Where a file here disagrees with a Library guide, the guide is the later, checked statement. Prices and availability are as seen on the date above.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| aluminium.json, titanium.json, steels-others.json | Per-alloy properties, availability and prices, with sources |
| alloy-corrections.json | A datasheet audit's corrections to those three files |
| alloys-final.json | The corrected, consistent-basis property table (spec minimum for tube where one exists) |
| tables.js, tables.md | The calculator and its output: weight, stiffness, bend strength, dent index and heat carried for a 60 in x 0.75 in staff tube at several walls |
| heated-fumes.json | Which metals, platings and coatings give off harmful fumes when heated, and at what temperature |
| wall-thickness.json, length-sizing.json | Tube walls and diameters makers use; staff length and how to size one |
| fuel-identities.json, fuel-use.json | Fuel names by region, safety-data-sheet identities and flash points; what spinners use and why |
| clothing-research-dossier.md | The research behind the fire clothing fabrics post |
| fire-props-scout-reports.json | Six read-only scout reports on what the engine has for fire props, effects and combustion, including 54 conflicts between data/chemistry and this research |

Not legal or engineering advice. None of the strength indices are a substitute for testing a real staff.
