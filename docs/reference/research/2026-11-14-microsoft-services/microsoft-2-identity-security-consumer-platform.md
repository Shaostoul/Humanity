# Microsoft services, part 2: identity, security, consumer and platform

What this file is: an inventory of Microsoft's identity, security, consumer and platform services,
built from Microsoft's own product and support pages, so HumanityOS can see what people rely on
Microsoft for. Productivity and communication (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, Word and the rest, and the
Microsoft 365 plans) are in the sibling file `microsoft-1-productivity-and-communication.md`.

Read on 14 November 2026 (Pacific).

Method: pages read with Node's `fetch` (and `curl` for robots files), identifying as
`Claude-User (research tool)`, then stripped to text and searched for the sentences quoted here.
Each host's robots.txt was read first, in a step of its own, before any other page of that host
(two exceptions, disclosed under "Robots.txt read": explore.microsoft.com and rewards.bing.com were
reached by an automatic redirect from www.microsoft.com before their robots files were read; both
robots files allow those pages, and redirects to new hosts were not followed after that). A web
search was used only to find support-page addresses; every fact below comes from the Microsoft
page named beside it. Quotations are exact (curly quotes normalised to straight). Prices that the
pages fill in by script (Entra P1 and P2, Game Pass, Microsoft 365 Copilot) could not be read and
are not given.

Dates: Microsoft's product pages show no date, so they are "undated page". Microsoft Learn pages
show a "Last updated" date, given where it appears. Support pages show no date on screen; their
page metadata date is given in brackets where present.

Plain words used below: "two-factor" means signing in needs a second proof (a phone approval or a
code) besides the password; a "passkey" is a sign-in key kept on your device and unlocked by face,
fingerprint or PIN, with no password to steal; "single sign-on" means one sign-in opens many apps;
a "verifiable credential" is a digital card (a diploma, an employee badge) signed by whoever issued
it, which you hold in a wallet app and show to others.
