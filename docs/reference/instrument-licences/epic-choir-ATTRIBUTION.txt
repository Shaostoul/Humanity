Changes: Derived work: segments of the VocalSet long tones / scales / arpeggios (straight, pp, forte, slow piano / forte; vowels a e i o u) cut, pitch-shifted by at most 2 semitones, their sustains extended from their own audio, level-matched, placed in a simulated hall and summed per note into section samples (one real singer per take).
