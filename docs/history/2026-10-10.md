## Shipped

- **v0.1469.0, reports the admins can check** (design section 8, step D; tagged late on 9
  October, after the 2026-10-09 note was written). Signed reports with
  the messages the reporter received as evidence, each checked against its sender's own
  signature, so a report can be neither forged nor pinned on someone else. A message carrying a
  file is never accepted as evidence: its envelope holds the file's key, which would let an
  admin open the file. Reports for admins and moderators in both apps; reports kept 90 days.
- **v0.1470.0, calls that keep your address private** (step E). One UDP port, 3478, with our
  own STUN responder and a closed call forwarder whose relayed addresses are virtual and which
  delivers only between people in the same call or room, so it cannot be pointed at an outside
  address. Both apps relay calls only and no longer ask Google for anything. Calls stay
  unavailable, and the apps say so, until the operator opens the port
  (`docs/admin/call-forwarder.md`).
- **Two dated findings.** `docs/reference/findings/2026-10-09-outside-help-lines.md` (the
  per-country help lines in `data/safety/outside_help.json`) and
  `docs/reference/findings/2026-10-10-report-duties-child-abuse-material.md` (18 U.S.C. 2258A and
  2258B, plus the UK and EU duties). From the second: reported posts now show admins neither a
  file nor a link (`without_files_or_links` in `src/relay/handlers/reports.rs`). Its three open
  decisions are the operator's and sit in PRIORITIES.
- **v0.1471.0, warnings and the recovery-phrase guard** (step F, design 10g). A short note under
  a stranger's message that asks for gift cards or a wire transfer, asks for a recovery phrase,
  pushes the chat to another app, rushes or asks for secrecy, or claims to be staff; the money
  and recovery-phrase notes also show for friends, because an account can be taken over. A
  stranger's links wait for Open. Matched only on the device, from
  `data/safety/warnings.json`, by one rule on both clients. And neither app sends a message
  holding 4 or more of your own recovery phrase's words in a row, with no send-anyway.

## Lessons

- **"Letters and digits" is not one thing across languages.** The spec first said the web class
  `\p{L}`; Rust's `char::is_alphanumeric` is the Alphabetic property, which keeps combining marks
  such as a Devanagari vowel sign inside a word where `\p{L}` splits it. The shared cases now
  hold one that tells them apart (case 17), so the two clients cannot drift silently.
- **Test the guard on the way people actually copy the thing.** Backup screens number the
  recovery phrase, so people paste "1. word 2. word". The guard's "words in a row" saw a number
  between every pair and let the whole phrase through. Found by the desktop builder, fixed on
  both clients by leaving out number-only words, each test seen failing without the fix.
- **A legal duty can sit inside a feature's evidence path.** Sending a DM as evidence sends the
  key to any file it carries. The report-duties research turned a design detail into a hard
  rule: no file evidence, and no files or links shown to admins from a reported post.
