- **v0.1424.0: in a shared world the host's clock wins** (operator

  summed and each crop's `planted_at` shifts with it, because farming
  keeps absolute stamps and a naive jump would have ripened or reset every
  garden on joining; sleep is refused there; 20 s without a sync hands the
  speed back and the host's date stays. Four tests, each seen red.
