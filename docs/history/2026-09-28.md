- **v0.1396.1: two food guides verified, jerky can be made again.** Drying

- **BUG-090 fixed: the home keeps its own time.** The survey of 26 September
  said the drawn sun ran on the wall clock; it did not (the planet has turned
  on the game clock since v0.878). The real cause: the spin is tied to the
  sun's azimuth, which creeps a full turn a year, while the station was placed
  by the game clock alone, so the longitude below the home, and the hour the
  deck saw noon, walked through the whole day over a year. It lined up by
  chance in late September. Now the station is re-phased by the same azimuth
  (`station::orbit::over_its_longitude`) and hangs over 122.3 W on every date,
  and the home's panels, grow-light timer, crop light, HUD clock aboard and
  wake notice read the home's local solar time. The game clock stays
  longitude 0's time so the time pinned in 482 of the 486 vantages keeps its meaning.

- **v0.1398.0: the banding report closed (HDR scene target increments 3
  and 4).** The code was built on 27 September and left unmerged at the usage
  cap. Finished today: the GPU tests re-run (7 of 7), three same-boot A/B
  plans (the float target moves still scenes by at most 2 codes; the one
  dither cuts the flat bands at every dark vantage with the means and the
  aurora comb held; about 0.03 to 0.06 ms in all at 2560x1387, against an
  estimate of 0.2 to 0.4), world entry with no panics, the vantage pins and
  the docs (`docs/design/hdr-scene-target.md` section 7, BUG-104).

- **v0.1399.0: a shelter works with its back to the wind.** Arc C's
  shelter item: which sides are walled now matters, not just how many. Under a
  roof the wind reaching the body is the share that comes in through the open
  sides facing into it (`ShelterCheck::wind_share`), so three walls shelter
  only when the open side faces away from the wind, as the survival manuals
  teach, and the HUD says how much of the wind gets in.

## Learned

- A survey's diagnosis is a hypothesis. BUG-090's effect was right and its
  mechanism wrong; reading the spin code before fixing saved building a
  second clock for the sky.
- The recipe sources lint counts only creatures that are actually spawned
  (`wild_spawns.ron`, `livestock.ron`), so a creature drop listed in
  `creatures.csv` is not a source by itself.
