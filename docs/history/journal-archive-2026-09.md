The march footprint now computes from ndc_step.y * tanf (its OWN rasterized Nyquist rate) instead of a hardcoded *4.0 - bit-identical at quarter res, and it means raising the resolution buys real field detail, not just a sharper upsample. What have we not tried adjusting yet?" - resolution was the untried lever and it is the largest single look change of the arc. ALSO SETTLED THIS ROUND: the sun-cone spiral is INNOCENT (polar decomposition of the direct-sun bisect, cone ON vs OFF: ringSD 10.77 vs 10.01, spokeSD 13.73 vs 13.50 - identical within content noise). The rosette remains uncaught; every renderer mechanism tested so far is cleared, and the measured mip-response drift (Blueprint 1) is the remaining named suspect.


## 2026-09-01

**Decision:** v0.1256.0: THE PRIMITIVE WAS A BALL - per-cloud shape frame. Every lobe was length(p-c)-r, a literal sphere; displacement/erosion/smooth-union only decorate a round object, which is why 20 fidelity increments never cured "cotton balls". cloud_v2_body now transforms the QUERY POINT into a per-cloud shape frame before cv2_cloud_sdf: rotate into a wind direction shared across 8-cell patches (streets), divide by axes (vertical squash 0.42-0.88 mapped from genus aspect x per-cloud variation, along-wind stretch 1.0-1.5, cross-wind = the reciprocal so the three axes multiply to 1 and VOLUME/areal coverage are untouched), then scale the returned distance by the smallest axis so it stays a conservative bound for the march SDF leap. Reject radius inflated by the stretch; envelope arithmetic checked against the 3x3 search bound (990 < 1100 at humilis max). Placement and lobes both live in the frame, so a cloud stretches as ONE object, not a string of stretched beads.

**Why:** Operator on v0.1255.1: "How can we make these clouds incredibly less spherical... still just giant cotton balls of slightly varying shape." Root cause is the primitive itself. LOCKSTEP: deliberately applied in the FIELD function, not inside cv2_cloud_sdf, so the CPU twin (cloud_primitives.rs) mirrors the cluster builder unchanged and all 8 of its tests stay valid. Rig: clouds render visibly flattened and wind-drawn, 12 FPS vs 13.3 baseline (near-free). ALSO from this round: full cloud resolution fixed the low-res planet from distance (operator confirmed). OPEN: performance (4-5 FPS at full res - the march is the whole frame budget, a cost pass is the next named item); pre-volumetric to volumetric coverage continuity; the rosette (every renderer mechanism cleared; mip-response drift/Blueprint 1 remains the one measured suspect).

Still reads as cotton balls per the operator, though "way better" ones.


## 2026-09-01

**Decision:** v0.1258.0: trapezoid step integration + THE ROSETTE UNIFIED WITH THE MEASURED MIP DRIFT. Shipped: the march integrates each step as a trapezoid over its two endpoint densities (dens_prev is already held) instead of a point sample times the step length - the exact integral of a linear ramp, halves the estimator variance, free, and softens the first step into a cloud (the binary opaque-or-clear edge). speck-alpha 2.04 -> 1.91 at unchanged cost.

**Why:** Operator described the artifact geometrically for the first time: "the bottom 45 degree cone beneath my feet is scrunching the clouds together... at a distance the textures look proper fluffy, closer to my feet they warp inwards", plus "the static is concentrated at the CENTER of the clouds". THE UNIFICATION: both quantities that set the march sampling scale are monotone in the angle from the NADIR - r_rate (dot of the radial with the ray) drives dt_vert, and foot = tm * pix_ang drives lodb/mip. So step size AND detail mip vary radially about the feet by construction. The v0.1252.4 workflow MEASURED that the rendered result is NOT invariant to that scale (carve response residual doubling per rung from mip 3, up to ~15 percent relative near threshold, eight uncompensated taps). Appearance varying with a quantity that varies radially about the crosshair IS the rosette/warp/scrunch. Blueprint 1 (per-mip histogram matching in the bake + width-table refit) is therefore the DIRECT cure for the operator oldest complaint, not a side quest. FOUR SUSPECTS ELIMINATED WITH NUMBERS this round: fine near-surface refine (-25% grain but +64% cost - rejected as a trade), fine erosion band ERODE2 zeroed (2.13 vs 2.04 - not the carrier), surface-centred sampling (2.39 - worse, the deterministic comb returns), interior turbulence 0.42->0.15 (1.99 - noise).


## 2026-09-01

**Decision:** v0.1259.0: BLUEPRINT 1 TESTED AND REFUTED (a real negative result, recorded in the shader so nobody retries it), plus the F10 shape-frame A/B toggle. Implemented per-mip CDF histogram matching in cloud_noise.rs renormalize_level (replacing the linear mean/sigma gain) exactly as the workflow designed it, then re-ran BOTH harnesses. Measured: coverage drift got WORSE at every meaningful level - mips 1-6 went +27/37/37/56/47/74% against the linear form 18/28/27/35/34/53%, and the degenerate 1-voxel level flipped -100% to +304%. The carve width harness then refit the table DOWN (0.005/0.005/0.005/0.005/0.005/0.01/0.02/0.005/0.005) and passed its own gate, but re-measuring coverage showed no recovery. REASON, now understood and written into the width-table comment: matching the GLOBAL distribution does not make a mip agree LOCALLY with the area-averaged truth, because thresholding a smoothed field is not the same as smoothing a thresholded field. The lever must be the CONSUMPTION side - the soft-hinge width fitted against COVERAGE rather than the mean, which is exactly what the standing note in clouds.rs coverage_vs_mip already says. Bake and width table reverted; carve_consistency gate green.

**Why:** Operator: "Let us proceed with all the fixes until I can test them. Do not forget we have the F10 dev menu to toggle things on/off." SHIPPED FOR TESTING: an F10 "Cloud shape frame" checkbox (plus showcase {cloud_shape:0}) that renders the pre-v0.1256 isotropic ball cluster, so the squash/wind-stretch work can be A/B compared live instead of from memory. The pad light7_color.w is now a BIT FIELD (bit 0 dither off, bit 1 shape off) and the dither test was corrected to read bit 0 only - otherwise disabling the shape frame would have silently disabled the dither too.

So the composite does not remove clouds; where clouds are missing, the MARCH genuinely produced no coverage. That relocates the splotch hunt to the march/field.
