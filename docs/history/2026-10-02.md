## Learned

- A backup job that logs "pulled OK" can be failing for weeks: its only check
  (a valid SQLite header) was true of the stale file it kept fetching. The
  2026-09-29 health check recorded it as OK. A backup's proof is its age and
  its hash against the source, not its format.
- Fixing one thing can arm another: making the Tasks page sign in would have
  made closing it sign the person out of voice and the game, because the
  relay treated one closed socket as the person leaving. The reviewer of the
  fix found it; a review after every parallel wave earns its cost.
