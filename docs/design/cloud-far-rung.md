## What it is

The speckles are the constructed bodies (`cloud_v2_body`, 41-cloud-bodies.wgsl:773) point-sampled at footprints larger than the clouds. `CLOUD_V2_FADE_LO/HI` = 3.9/4.0 (40-clouds.wgsl:150-151) keeps the built body at full weight for every footprint under 15 km, so from 873 km every sample of a 300 m to 8 km object field on 1.1 to 3.2 km cells (`cv2_cell_km`, 41-cloud-bodies.wgsl:40-43) is a coin flip: inside a lobe (opaque at 45/km) or clear, printed as one full-white texel. Measured on the 873 km captures (greyscale > 200, central 80 percent): High 31.0 percent white as coherent sheets, Ultra day-0 25.3 percent as sugar grain, Ultra eco0 9.1 percent as dots, eco1 5.2 percent (fewer coin flips, a field move, not a fix); grain 2.19 (High) against 2.79 to 2.98 (Ultra). The noise body is already band-limited (mip chain plus the compact-support hinge, 40-clouds.wgsl:2467-2486); the built body has no prefiltered representation at all. Increment 4 gives it one.

The far rung is a planet-fixed PROFILE: per lattice cell and height bin, the cloud FRACTION `f` (areal fraction of the cell holding cloud anywhere in the bin's height range), the MEAN density `G` of the same field the march renders, and the COLUMN `C` above each bin, baked at the cell's own footprint into an RGBA8 atlas over six nested toroidal (clipmap) windows on an absolute equirect lattice plus one global equirect map with a real mip pyramid, and read by the march with 4-tap loads chosen by the same `lodb` that picks every noise mip. The profile share is integrated in TRANSMITTANCE by a continuous clumped-medium law that is a property of the MEDIUM, not of the step (fraction plus in-cloud density, max overlap within one cloud element, random beyond), never by a per-ray switch and never through a plain mean (a 30 percent cumulus field fed as a mean into Beer-Lambert is opaque: the white-veil class). At `w = 1` the march skips the density call AND the sun ladder/cache entirely; that is where the orbit cost goes. The same global map redraws the Low sheet.

The built part of the bake is ANALYTIC: each placed cloud is an ellipse per height from a runtime CALIBRATION table (per archetype and cloud-relative height: equivalent-circle radius ratio and mean in-cloud density, point-tested once on the shipped SDF), so no per-texel SDF work exists anywhere in the bake.

Dev pad `light2_color` (byte offset 240 in `CameraUniforms`, 00-bindings-vertex.wgsl:27; declared in the four shader copies of the struct, read by no shader, written by no Rust: grep verified) carries the ground cell, the knob and the flags; no bit of `light7_color.w` is used (it is full: bits 0..23, mod.rs:3916-3945; note bit 17 IS taken by the body cluster cache at mod.rs:3945 despite the stale "bit 17 is still free" comment at 40-clouds.wgsl:779-780). Knob 0 = off, today's field bit-identical (the A/B twin). Showcase key `cloud_profile`, F10 checkbox "Cloud profile, the far rung (off = point-sampled bodies at every footprint, for A/B)" plus a level combo, renderer field `cloud_profile_knob: i32`, GuiState `cloud_dev_profile_knob: i32`, the lib.rs mirror line beside `state.renderer.cloud_light = state.gui_state.cloud_dev_light;` (lib.rs:18557). Default 1 once gates G1 to G6 pass, else 0.

## Diagnosis of record and the decisions

1. Footprint at 873 km. The temporal march passes `ndc_step.y * tanf` to `cloud_march_core` (45-cloud-temporal.wgsl:294-296; the comment at 288-293 explains it tracks `cloud_res_div`); `cloud_pix_ang_screen()` (40-clouds.wgsl:647-650, pad `light5_cone_inner.z` written at mod.rs:3173-3176 from `config.height`) is read by exactly ONE caller: the direct shell path in `cloud_layer_volumetric` (40-clouds.wgsl:3335), which runs only when the temporal map is not armed (`material.params2.w < 3.5`, else the fragment discards at 3312-3321). Neither the Medium march (`cloud_layer_march`, 1520, its own loop with `g_sigma_med`, never `cloud_march_core`) nor the Low sheet (`cloud_layer_flat`, 1392, no footprint at all) reads it. Consequence: the rig and the operator exercise different profile levels, so every gate runs at BOTH `cloud_res` 1 and 4, each as an explicit pinned vantage (A15). Footprint law: `foot_km = slant_km * pix_ang`, `pix_ang = 2 tan(fov/2) / rows` = 1.4432e-3 (full, 1387 rows), 2.8864e-3 (half), 5.7729e-3 (quarter) rad at fov 90.05 (the rig's 2560 x 1387 captures; nadir pixel (1280, 693) at look 0, `f = 693 px`). 873 km nadir: 1.26 km (lodb 0.33) full, 5.04 km (2.33) quarter; limb slant 3448 km: 4.98 km (2.32) full, 19.9 km (4.3) quarter.
2. Handoff range. AMENDED (D3, v0.1293): the march resolves the field until the HORIZONTAL footprint approaches the cloud width ONLY IF its vertical sampling resolves the layer, and from above it does not: the built layer is based at the slab base (41-cloud-bodies.wgsl, the flat base at up_m = 0), the vertical reject in cloud_v2_body leaves the SDF at its 1e9 sentinel so the stride is inert from above, the comb is 522 m (step_near) to 928 m (the vertical ceiling), and the march's last sample lands exactly on the base plane where density is 0; so only one comb sample per ray can find a humilis (hit probability h / 928 = 0.11 to 0.22) at EVERY altitude, and the found ones are skimmed by the economy floors. The shipped baseline's 0.9 percent at 873 km and the 1.2 percent at 250 km inside the HARD ring were this vertical comb, not the footprint. The cure is the built-body top bound (the vertical gap to the admitted density region published as the SDF lower bound so the stride finds every cloud whose disc the sample is in) plus the in-cloud floor capped at a quarter of the found cloud's height, behind the D3 dev bit (light2_color.w bit 12); the band below stands as the horizontal criterion once the march resolves the layer. The jitters are FROZEN (v0.1253.2), so nothing reconstructs sub-footprint content and band-limiting must happen in the field once the footprint approaches the cloud width; the v0.1233 window (250 m to 1 km) is the band: `CLOUD_FR_LOD_LO = -2.0`, `CLOUD_FR_LOD_HI = 0.0` in the dithered coordinate `lodf = lodb + g_lod_jitter * 0.35` (40-clouds.wgsl:2263).
3. Consumption law. The fraction form with max overlap within one element and random overlap beyond it, written as a property of the medium (A7): `tau_elem = sigma_v * D_in * L_elem`, `T_pf = pow(1 - w_pf * f * (1 - exp(-tau_elem)), seg / L_elem)`. Exponents add across any subdivision of a traversal, so the result is independent of the step law by construction (the v1 form `tau_c = sigma * D_in * min(seg, L_elem)` was O(seg^2) for seg < L_elem and halved the extinction on every halving of the step: refuted, replaced). No per-ray state beyond `trans`, no column fold, no column id.
4. Lattice. ABSOLUTE equirect with EQUAL angular spacing in longitude and latitude, no anchor, no cos(lat) scaling (A4). `cloud_v2_body` keys its cells on `lat = asin(clamp(dir.y))`, `lon = atan2(-dir.z, dir.x)` (41-cloud-bodies.wgsl:793-794) but scales its longitude cells by `cos(lat)` (797-799); the profile lattice deliberately does NOT, so east-west cells shrink by cos(lat) in km at high latitude, a window spans less ground east-west there and the tap hands to the global map (which has mips) sooner: a blur, never a speckle, no discontinuity at the pole, and the polar enumeration blow-up of the v1 lattice does not exist (see A3 below: on Earth the cap is never reached).
5. Windows are TOROIDAL (clipmap), no re-anchor (A5). Every level's grid shares the origin, so nesting holds; moving the ground point by one cell exposes one column or row, baked the same frame.
6. Bake of the built part: analytic per placed cloud from a calibration table (A1), `wa` at each enumerated cv2 cell centre (A2), stride subsampling past the cap with a Poisson union (A3). The v1 per-texel SDF point tests are gone (they cost 4e9 SDF builds per level-5 bake; refuted).
7. Storage: f, G AND the column C (A14). Three quantities, nine slices per level. The v1 "sum six G fetches at read time" is gone.
8. Window size `CLOUD_FR_NX = 512` stands; the descent IoU gate decides whether NX becomes 1024 (4x memory).
9. Global pyramid: box-filtered mip chain in real texture mips over the global region only; read by two integer-mip fetches lerped in the shader because the group's sampler has `mipmap_filter: Nearest` (A11; mod.rs:1220-1229).
10. Cost bar: `gpu.cloud_screen` at F2 within 1.5x of High at the same camera (High 13.376 ms on disk, `.probe-rig/sweeps/20260905-015127/tmp-P-orbit-high-costs.json`) AND down 50 percent from the shipped 30.6 ms (`.probe-rig/sweeps/20260905-210918/tmp-L2-orbit-eco1-costs.json`); predicted under 10 ms. Cadence is TIME based and the bake cost is gated on its MAX over a moving fixture (A6).
11. Baseline. The day-0 to v0.1288 orbit picture change is unattributed (the tmp 873 km cells are gone from `vantages.json`; no 873 km vantage exists there today): fixture F2 with pins and a bits-16/17 off/on pair before the baseline capture is a precondition.
12. Group 3 wiring (A8, corrected against the code): `self.cloud_temporal` (the `CloudTemporal` octa-map struct) is initialised `None` at mod.rs:1863 and NEVER assigned `Some` anywhere (the octa map was deleted in v0.1261), so the transparent shell draw's override at mod.rs:4472-4483 always falls through to `default_texture_bind_group` today, and the shell fragment discards while the temporal map is armed (40-clouds.wgsl:3312-3321). The temporal ACCUMULATION groups are `CloudScreen.groups` (cloud_temporal.rs:174-175, built by `build_albedo_group_from_view`), which no pass binds today. The mechanism A8 decided stands: a b14 override on the one entry-list builder, every cloud-side group rebuilt with the profile atlas at binding 14, no layout change, 16 entries at every site.

## The data: lattice, bins, texels, atlas (Rust owns the numbers; WGSL reads them from the pads and its own constants block)

Lattice (A4). Level `L = 0..5`, cell `c_L = CLOUD_FR_CELL0_KM * 2^L` km = 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 km of ARC (north-south everywhere; east-west c_L * cos(lat)). `cell_rad_L = c_L / planet_km` radians (Earth level 0: 3.9240e-5). Cells around the planet `N_I(L) = floor(2 pi planet_km / c_L)` (Earth: 160120, 80060, 40030, 20015, 10007, 5003), rows `N_J(L) = floor(pi planet_km / c_L)`; BOTH languages compute these floors in f32 arithmetic from `planet_km` so they are bit-identical (a Rust test asserts the f64 and f32 floors agree for Earth). Cell `(I, J)`, `I` in `[0, N_I)`, `J` in `[0, N_J)`, is centred at `lon_c = (I + 0.5) * cell_rad_L - pi`, `lat_c = (J + 0.5) * cell_rad_L - pi/2`. Index of a direction: `I = clamp(floor((lon + pi) / cell_rad_L), 0, N_I - 1)`, `J = clamp(floor((lat + pi/2) / cell_rad_L), 0, N_J - 1)` with `lat = asin(clamp(dirp.y, -1, 1))`, `lon = atan2(-dirp.z, dirp.x)`. The strip `[N_I * cell_rad_L, 2 pi)` at the date line belongs to no cell and reads cell `N_I - 1` (a seam of at most one cell, accepted, same as the global's clamp).

Ground cell. Rust computes in f64 from `p_l` (the cloud fill block's planet-local render-unit vector, lib.rs:12266, the same frame as `CloudLightFrame.ground_local_m`): `lat_g = asin(y / |p|)`, `lon_g = atan2(-z, x)`, `ground_I_0 = floor((lon_g + pi) / cell_rad_0)`, `ground_J_0 = floor((lat_g + pi/2) / cell_rad_0)`, RAW indices in `[0, N_I(0))` and `[0, N_J(0))`, never unwrapped (unwrapping across the date line drifts the coarse levels by up to one cell per crossing because `N_I(L) * 2^L != N_I(0)`; a crossing is handled by a full refill instead, see the scroll rule). Per level, in both languages: `ground_I_L = floor(ground_I_0 / 2^L)`, `ground_J_L = floor(ground_J_0 / 2^L)` (exact in f32 for integers below 2^24; WGSL: `floor(pad.x / exp2(f32(L)))`).

Windows (A5). Level L covers window-frame cells `I_abs` in `[I0_L, I0_L + 512)`, `J_abs` in `[J0_L, J0_L + 512)` with `I0_L = ground_I_L - 256`, `J0_L = ground_J_L - 256`. `I_abs` may lie outside `[0, N_I)`: its planet cell is `pmod(I_abs, N_I)`. `J_abs` outside `[0, N_J)` is void: the bake writes 0 there and no read ever addresses it (a real latitude always indexes an existing row). Storage inside a slice: `x = x0_s + pmod(I_abs, 512)`, `y = y0_s + pmod(J_abs, 512)` with `pmod(a, n) = ((a mod n) + n) mod n` (toroidal: a cell's storage position never changes while it stays in the window). Nesting: every level's grid shares the origin `(-pi, -pi/2)`, so level L's cell `(I, J)` is exactly the 2x2 block `(2I..2I+1, 2J..2J+1)` of level L-1.

Bins. `CLOUD_FR_NZ = 12` height bins in normalized slab height `h = (r - g_cloud_rb) / (g_cloud_rt - g_cloud_rb)`, bin k = `[k/12, (k+1)/12)` (967 m on Earth, the march's 928 m far vertical step). Per bin: `f_k` = areal fraction of the cell with density above `CLOUD_STEP_INTERIOR_GATE` (0.02, 40-clouds.wgsl:497) at ANY sampled height inside the bin (a union over heights, so a 300 m humilis in a 967 m bin counts once); `G_k` = mean density over the cell AND over the bin's sampled heights; `C_k = sum_{j > k} G_j` (the column above bin k, exclusive; `C_11 = 0`); `T = sum_{j = 0..11} G_j = C_0 + G_0` (the whole column). `D_in = G / f` is then the in-cloud density times the fraction of the bin height the cloud fills, which makes the element law exact for thin clouds.

Texels (A14). RGBA8Unorm. Per level, nine slices `p = 0..8`: pair slices `p = 0..5` hold `(f_k, G_k, f_{k+1}, G_{k+1})` for `k = 2p`; column slices `p = 6, 7, 8` (`q = p - 6`) hold `(C_{4q}, C_{4q+1}, C_{4q+2}, C_{4q+3})` for `q = 0, 1` and `(C_8, C_9, C_10, T)` for `q = 2` (`C_11` is identically zero, so its channel carries `T` instead: the read then needs at most two column fetches, exactly as A14 budgets). `f` and `G` are stored linearly (both in [0, 1]). Column values (`C_k`, `T`, in slab-bin units, up to 12) are stored as `enc(C) = sqrt(clamp(C / CLOUD_FR_COL_SCALE, 0, 1))`, decoded `dec(v) = v * v * CLOUD_FR_COL_SCALE` (`CLOUD_FR_COL_SCALE = 12.0`; the square root gives the low-tau end, where the light changes, 8-bit steps of 0.008 in tau instead of 2.0). Slice `s = L * 9 + p` (0..53) sits at `x0_s = (s mod 12) * 512`, `y0_s = (s / 12) * 512`: 12 slices per atlas row, five rows, window band `6144 x 2560`. Row 4 holds `s = 48..53` (level 5's slices 3..8) in columns 0..5; columns 6..11 of row 4 (`x >= 3072, y in [2048, 2560)`) are spare at mip 0 (nothing is stored there; the calibration lives in mips 1 and 2 of the window band, see the bake section).

Global. One equirect map, `CLOUD_FR_GLOBAL_W x H = 2048 x 1024` texels: global cell `global_km = 2 pi planet_km / 2048` of arc (19.55 km on Earth; computed in both languages from the planet radius, never a constant), `CLOUD_FR_GLOBAL_NZ = 4` pooled bins of three slab bins each (`fp_q` = union of `f_{3q..3q+2}`, `Gp_q` = mean of `G_{3q..3q+2}`), `Cp_q = sum_{j > q} Gp_j` (pooled-bin units, `Cp_3 = 0`), `Tp = sum_q Gp_q`. Texel `(i, j)` centre: `lon = (i + 0.5) / 2048 * 2 pi - pi`, `lat = pi/2 - (j + 0.5) / 1024 * pi` (row 0 = north, matching the weather map's `w_uv`, 40-clouds.wgsl:1767). Three slices in the global row `y0 = CLOUD_FR_GLOBAL_Y0 = 2560`: pair slice 0 at x 0 `(fp_0, Gp_0, fp_1, Gp_1)`, pair slice 1 at x 2048 `(fp_2, Gp_2, fp_3, Gp_3)`, column slice at x 4096 `(Cp_0, Cp_1, Cp_2, Tp)` (same encoding, same scale). The global region is `6144 x 1024` and fills its row.

Mips. The atlas texture has `CLOUD_FR_GLOBAL_MIPS = 7` mip levels; mips 1..6 hold the 2x2 box average of the GLOBAL REGION only: at mip m the region is at origin `(0, 2560 >> m)`, size `(6144 >> m, 1024 >> m)`, slice x offsets `(2048 >> m) * p`; `2560 = 5 * 2^9` and `3584 = 7 * 2^9`, so every region and slice edge stays texel-aligned at every mip. The window band's mip texels are never written or read by the march, EXCEPT the two small calibration areas (bake section). The mip fragment decodes column channels before averaging and re-encodes after (averaging square roots would under-read coarse columns).

Atlas. `CLOUD_FR_ATLAS_W = 6144`, `CLOUD_FR_ATLAS_H = 3584`, RGBA8Unorm, 7 mips. Mip 0 = 88.1 MB (`6144 * 3584 * 4`); the global region's mips 1..6 = 8.4 MB; the two written together = 96.5 MB. wgpu allocates the full-extent chain, 117.4 MB, of which 20.9 MB (the window band's mips) is allocated and never written. Usage `RENDER_ATTACHMENT | TEXTURE_BINDING | COPY_SRC` (dev dump). Views: `view_all` (all 7 mips) for the march, the shell and the Low sheet; `view_mip[m]`, `m = 0..6` (one mip each), as attachments and as read sources. Rides group 3 binding 14 `tree_atlas_tex: texture_2d<f32>` (00-bindings-vertex.wgsl:189; read only by the vegetation branches of 90-fragment-main.wgsl:1392 and 2329, never by cloud code; binding 0 keeps the sun atlas). NO bind-group-layout change: `texture_bind_group_layout` (pipeline.rs:237) keeps 16 entries (0..15). Sampler for the global reads: the group's `albedo_sampler` (mod.rs:1220-1229: mag/min Linear, mipmap Nearest, u Repeat, v ClampToEdge); every global read is clamped to texel centres inside its own slice at its own mip so the hardware bilinear never crosses a slice edge and the u wrap is never reached. Window reads use `textureLoad` (integer texel coordinates, level 0, no sampler), the same primitive the water tile uses (00-bindings-vertex.wgsl:554-557).

Lean variant if the IoU gate wants NX 1024 or memory bites: 8 bins (4 pair + 2 column slices per level) cuts the window band to 1536 rows.

## Bake pass (Rust owns the passes, rects and cadence; WGSL owns the fragments)

### Calibration (A1): `fs_cloud_profile_calib` and `fs_cloud_profile_calib_reduce`

Run once, and re-run whenever the tier (`material.params.y`), the interior saturation (`camera.light5_color.w`), the wide-edge bit (pad bit 6) or the component bisect index (pad bits 13-15) changes (Rust watches these; a re-run orders a FILL of every active level and a fast global pass). Two fullscreen-triangle stages, both through `build_cloud_fullscreen_pipeline` (pipeline.rs:519-556) with one RGBA8Unorm target, both timed under their own pass timer `gpu.cloud_profile_calib` so the bake gate's MAX never counts them:

Stage 1, attachment `view_mip[2]`, group 3 = `default_texture_bind_group` (it reads only the noise volumes), scissor `(CLOUD_FR_CALIB_STAGE_X0, CLOUD_FR_CALIB_STAGE_Y0, 32, 32)` in mip-2 texels. Texel `(x, y)`: `row = x - 768` (0..31), `arch_i = (y - 512) / 8`, `seed_s = (y - 512) mod 8`. The canonical cloud: `u_s = (seed_s + 0.5) / 8` (stratified over the power-law size draw, so the eight clouds ARE the size distribution), `arch = cv2_arch(arch_i, u_s)` (no growth), `seed = cv2_hash(vec2(f32(seed_s), f32(arch_i)), 7.0) * 4096`, unit shape frame `sx = sy = sz = 1`, `width = arch.width_m`, `height_m = width * arch.aspect`, `br = width * 0.5 + CLOUD_V2_RIND_M`, `A_box = 4 br^2`. Height of this row: `y_local = (row + 0.5) / 32 * CLOUD_FR_CALIB_YMAX * height_m` (rows span 0 to 1.5 cloud heights; the body rejects samples above `height * sy + br`, 41-cloud-bodies.wgsl:953-955, and 1.5 covers that for every archetype). Over the `64 x 64` grid `(gx, gy)` in `[-br, br]^2` (cell centres), `local_m = (gx, y_local, gy)`, `d = cv2_cloud_sdf(local_m, seed, arch)` (uncached; `g_sun_profile = 0` so all lobes build), `dens = cv2_density_tail(d, y_local, height_m, 0.0, 0.5, 0.5, CLOUD_V2_RIND_M, sat)` (displacement zero-mean, Worley mean 0.5, turbulence mean 0.5, the EYE's rind, `sat = clamp(camera.light5_color.w, 0, 1)`; the wide-edge experiment is honoured inside the tail as today). `n_in = count(dens > 0.02)`, `A = n_in / 4096 * A_box`, `rho_r = sqrt(A / pi) / width`, `Dbar = sum(dens over in-cloud points) / max(n_in, 1)`. Write `(rho_r, Dbar, 0, 1)`.

Stage 2, attachment `view_mip[1]`, group 3 = `group_mip_src[2]` (b14 = `view_mip[2]`), scissor `(CLOUD_FR_CALIB_X0, CLOUD_FR_CALIB_Y0, 32, 4)` in mip-1 texels. Texel `(x, y)`: `row = x - 1536`, `arch_i = y - 1024`; average the eight staging texels `textureLoad(tree_atlas_tex, vec2(768 + row, 512 + arch_i * 8 + s), 0)` (level 0 of the bound mip-2 view) over `s = 0..7`: plain means of `rho_r` and `Dbar`. Write `(rho_r_mean, Dbar_mean, 0, 1)`. Rust raises flag bit 8 after this frame.

Both areas live in the window band's mip levels, which nothing else writes (the global's mip-1 region starts at row 1280 and its mip-2 region at row 640, both below these areas). Cost: 1024 fragments x 4096 SDF evaluations, once: a few to ten milliseconds on the 4070.

### The bake fragment `fs_cloud_profile_bake`

In 45-cloud-temporal.wgsl beside `fs_cloud_light_bake` (402-481), `vs_cloud_screen` fullscreen triangle, one RGBA8Unorm target = `view_mip[0]`, group 3 = `group_mip_src[1]` (b14 = `view_mip[1]`, which holds the calibration table; mip 0 is this pass's attachment and may not also be sampled). Bound like the sun bake (mod.rs:4083-4092: camera, the cloud shell's object slot, the cloud material). Decode from `@builtin(position)` (pixel centre, truncate):

Two new publishes in `cloud_carve` (40-clouds.wgsl:2467-2486): `g_cloud_frac = clamp((zc + 1) * 0.5, 0, 1)` (the compact hinge's own fraction, zero cost) and `g_cloud_carve_pt = carve` (the pre-erosion carve), both `var<private>` beside `g_cloud_carve` (1895).

BUILT part (Ultra only, `material.params.y >= 2.5`, `arch_i = cv2_arch_index(tc_c) >= 0`; A1, A2, A3):

```

coslat_c = max(cos(lat_c), 0.05)                                  // the body's own clamp, 41-cloud-bodies.wgsl:797
dl = 0.5 * cell_rad_L   (window)   |   dl = pi / 2048   (global)  // the profile cell's angular half-extent, both axes

```

The shipped body only ever searches the 3x3 neighbourhood of a sample's cell (41-cloud-bodies.wgsl:811-813), so no cloud beyond one cv2 cell of the profile cell's extent can touch it; the one-cell margin above is exact, not conservative. Stride (A3): `stride = 1` if `N_cand <= CLOUD_FR_MAX_CV2` else `ceil(sqrt(N_cand / 512))`, with a per-texel hash phase `ph = floor(cv2_hash(vec2(f32(I), f32(J_abs)), 41.0) * stride)` applied to both axes (`ci = ci_lo + ph + stride * a`, `cj = cj_lo + ph + stride * b`). On Earth with these grids the cap is never reached (worst case is the global row at the equator for humilis, `(ceil(19.55 / 1.1) + 2)^2 = 400 < 512`; the equal-angle lattice only shrinks the east-west extent toward the poles), so stride is 1 everywhere on Earth and Rust's dev counter (below) reads 0; a larger planet is where it engages.

For each candidate `(ci, cj)`: `wa_i = clamp(cloud_alpha_from_field(cloud_weather_adv(dir_i, t, seed, wind_ang, wlod), coverage) + reg.cover_bias, 0, 1)` at the cv2 cell's OWN centre direction `dir_i` from `lon_i = (ci + 0.5) * cell_rad_cv2 / coslat_c`, `lat_i = (cj + 0.5) * cell_rad_cv2` (A2: presence is a threshold on `wa`, one value would flip whole clusters); `pl = cv2_place(ci, cj, arch_i, wa_i, g)` (the shared placement, below). If `!pl.present` continue. Per (bin k, height s) of this texel's bin set:

```

acc_union_ks *= (1 - a_i);  acc_sumA_ks += a_i;  acc_sumAD_ks += a_i * Dbar

After all candidates, per (k, s): `f_b,ks = 1 - acc_union_ks` (stride 1) or `f_b,ks = 1 - exp(-stride^2 * acc_sumA_ks)` (Poisson form when stride > 1); `G_b,ks = f_b,ks * acc_sumAD_ks / max(acc_sumA_ks, 1e-4)` (never exceeds `f_b`). Methods:

- `plan(frame)`: the active level range from the footprint law: `foot_min = max(alt - slab_top, 0) * pix_ang_march` km, `foot_max = (sqrt(max((R + alt)^2 - R^2, 0)) + sqrt((R + top)^2 - R^2)) * pix_ang_march`; level L is active iff `[L - 1, L + 1)` meets `[log2(foot_min) - LOD0, log2(foot_max) - LOD0]` (LOD0 = -2; empty above about 11,000 km at full res and 2,770 km at quarter; the forced level, knob 2..7, is always active). Global: first use, `|cloud_t - t_ref| > CLOUD_FR_GLOBAL_REREF_S` (a clock JUMP: the rolling pass restarts `t_ref` every 60 s, so only a scrub or pin change trips it), `weather_gen` change, `|coverage - coverage_ref| > 0.02`, `type_pin` change, tier change, knob class change (1/8 vs 9) each start a FAST pass (`pass_fast = true`, spread over `CLOUD_FR_REFRESH_S`); `valid` stays as it was during a re-reference (the old map serves until the new pass completes), except on first use.
- `pads() -> [f32; 4]` = `(ground_I_0, ground_J_0, knob, flags)`; `flags` from the valid bits.
- `take_bake_rects(dt_s) -> Vec<(u32, u32, u32, u32)>` for this frame, in this order: (1) scroll rects for every active valid level: for `dI = I0_new - I0_prev > 0` the columns `I_abs in [I0_prev + 512, I0_new + 512)`, for `dI < 0` the columns `[I0_new, I0_prev)`, each as storage runs `x = pmod(I_abs, 512)` (one or two contiguous runs per slice when the run wraps 512), height 512, for each of the level's 9 slices; rows likewise, width 512; (2) fill rects: `min(CLOUD_FR_FILL_ROWS, 512 - fill_cursor)` storage rows at `y = fill_cursor` for each of the 9 slices, advancing the cursor; when it reaches 512 the level becomes `valid`; (3) refresh rects for every valid active level: `n = ceil(512 * dt_s / CLOUD_FR_REFRESH_S)` rows (never more than `CLOUD_FR_REFRESH_ROWS_MAX` = 16; D5: a TIME-based cadence is a per-FRAME tax at low frame rates, because a fixed fraction per second becomes a large fraction per frame when the frame is 250 ms: at the rig's 4 fps the bake ran 16 to 18 ms every frame. The cost is now bounded per frame and the refresh period stretches instead, which clouds do not notice) at the refresh cursor, wrapping; (4) the global: `n = ceil(1024 * dt_s / (pass_fast ? CLOUD_FR_REFRESH_S : CLOUD_FR_GLOBAL_REFRESH_S))` rows at the pass cursor across all three global slices (width 6144), never more than `CLOUD_FR_GLOBAL_ROWS_MAX` = 8 per frame (D5); when the cursor completes 1024 rows, set `mips_pending`, `valid = true`, `pass_fast = false`, `t_ref = cloud_t`. ```
fn cloud_profile_level(L: i32, lon: f32, lat: f32, h: f32) -> ProfileLevel {   // ok, f, G, tau_above, tau_below, w_edge
    let cell_rad = CLOUD_FR_CELL0_KM * exp2(f32(L)) / planet_km
    let NI = floor(2pi * planet_km / (CLOUD_FR_CELL0_KM * exp2(f32(L))));  let NJ = floor(pi * planet_km / (...))
    let gI = floor(camera.light2_color.x / exp2(f32(L)));  let gJ = floor(camera.light2_color.y / exp2(f32(L)))
    let u = (lon + pi) / cell_rad - 0.5;  let v = (lat + pi/2) / cell_rad - 0.5
    var du = u - gI;  if du >= 0.5 * NI { du -= NI } else if du < -0.5 * NI { du += NI }      // date line
    let dv = v - gJ
    if !(level_valid(L) && du >= -256 && du < 255 && dv >= -256 && dv < 255) { return not ok }
    let i0 = floor(du); let fu = du - i0; let j0 = floor(dv); let fv = dv - j0
    let m = max(abs(du), abs(dv)) / 255.0;  let w_edge = smoothstep(1 - CLOUD_FR_BLEND_FRAC, 1, m)
    // storage coordinates of the four taps (J clamped to existing rows)
    x(i) = x0_s + pmod(gI + i0 + i, 512);  y(j) = y0_s + pmod(clamp(gJ + j0 + j, 0, NJ - 1), 512)
    // bins
    let fk = clamp(h * 12 - 0.5, 0, 11); let k0 = floor(fk); let k1 = min(k0 + 1, 11); let wk = fk - k0
    let kb = clamp(floor(h * 12), 0, 11);  let frac_above = (kb + 1) - h * 12          // fraction of bin kb above the sample
    // fetch pair slice(s) k0/2 (and k1/2 if different): bilinear over the four taps, then mix by wk -> f, G, and G_kb
    // fetch column slice kb/4 -> C_kb (decoded); T from column slice 2 channel w (a second column fetch unless kb/4 == 2)
    let dz = (g_cloud_rt - g_cloud_rb) / 12.0
    tau_above = sigma_v * dz * (C_kb + G_kb * frac_above)
    tau_below = sigma_v * dz * (T - C_kb - G_kb * frac_above)
    return ok
}
```

Bilinear on encoded column channels: decode each tap first, then blend (the encoding is not linear). One or two pair fetches and one or two column fetches per level, four loads each.

```
fn cloud_profile_tap(dirp: vec3<f32>, h: f32, lodb: f32, knob: i32) -> ProfileTap {   // ok, f, G, tau_above, tau_below, level
    lon, lat from dirp
    var lv = clamp(lodb - CLOUD_FR_LOD0, 0, 5)
    if knob in 2..7 { lv = f32(knob - 2) }                      // forced: one level, no blend
    var La = i32(floor(lv)); var Lb = min(La + 1, 5); var wl = lv - f32(La)
    if knob == 8 { wl = 0 }                                       // HARD: floor level only
    // arithmetic walk (no fetch): the first level >= La whose window contains the sample; likewise from Lb
    La = walk(La); Lb = walk(Lb)      // walk returns 6 = "the global" when no window level up to 5 contains it
    if La >= Lb { wl = 0; Lb = select(6, walk(La + 1), La < 5) }   // D1: the collapse is the COMMON case (floor(lv)'s own window does not contain the sample); re-walk to the NEXT containing window, the global only when none up to 5 contains it
    // at most TWO window fetches (La and Lb) plus the global. D1 (2026-09-06): the shipped form `Lb = La` here handed La's whole
    // edge band to the GLOBAL (a different estimator, 15 to 38 percent more coverage on the same ground), which printed every
    // window rim as a bright strip and a step down into the next level's pure interior: the window-edge frames. Never revert to it.
    ra = (La <= 5) ? cloud_profile_level(La, ...) : none
    let ea = select(1.0, ra.w_edge, ra.ok); if knob == 8 { ea = 0 }   // ea BEFORE the rb fetch and AFTER the HARD override
    // rb is fetched only when it can carry weight (wl > 0, or La has an edge band to hand to it); with wl = 0 and ea = 0 the
    // old fetch produced w_rb = 0 and w_g += 0, so skipping it is value-neutral, and HARD never fetches rb.
    rb = (Lb <= 5 && Lb != La && (wl > 0 || ea > 0)) ? cloud_profile_level(Lb, ...) : none;  let eb = select(1.0, rb.w_edge, rb.ok); if knob == 8 { eb = 0 }
    // hand-off weights (the sum over {ra, rb, global} is 1 by construction):
    //   La's edge band hands to rb (the next CONTAINING level, fetched whenever wl > 0 or ea > 0, after the HARD override has zeroed ea); only level 5's band, or a sample no window contains, hands to the global.
    //   HARD knob: ea = eb = 0 (the arithmetic walk already guarantees the fetched level contains the sample).
    var share_a = 1 - wl;  var share_b = wl;  var w_g = 0.0
    if La == 6 { share_a = 0; share_b = 0; w_g = 1 }                       // both walks ended at the global
    let w_ra = share_a * (1 - ea);  let handed_a = share_a * ea
    if rb.ok { share_b += handed_a } else { w_g += handed_a }
    let w_rb = share_b * (1 - eb);  w_g += share_b * eb
    g = (w_g > 0) ? cloud_profile_global(lon, lat, h, lodb) : none
    if w_g > 0 && !g.ok {                                                     // global wanted but not baked yet
        if w_ra + w_rb <= 0 { return not ok }                                 // -> w_pf = 0 for this sample
        renormalize: w_ra /= (w_ra + w_rb); w_rb /= (w_ra + w_rb); w_g = 0
    }
    f = w_ra * ra.f + w_rb * rb.f + w_g * g.f;   G, tau_above, tau_below likewise
    level = w_ra * f32(La) + w_rb * f32(Lb) + w_g * 6.0                        // channel 11 = level / 6
}
```

## Units

The lattice is ANGULAR: radians of the planet-local unit direction, `lat = asin(clamp(dirp.y, -1, 1))`, `lon = atan2(-dirp.z, dirp.x)`, the lines `cloud_v2_body` uses (41-cloud-bodies.wgsl:793-794) minus its cos-scaling of longitude; `cell_rad_L = CLOUD_FR_CELL0_KM * 2^L / planet_km` with `planet_km = material.params2.z` (WGSL, 40-clouds.wgsl:429-432) and `d.radius / 1000` (Rust, `CloudProfileFrame.radius_km`). The read needs only `dirp`, `h` and `lodb`; the only p-space quantities anywhere are the bake's sample point `dir_c * r_s` (drawn-shell units, 1 unit = `visual_scale * shell_ratio` render units, km = units / `g_cloud_upkm`), `dz` (one bin in p-units) and the element lengths (`km * g_cloud_upkm`). No f64 anchor in p-units, no planet-scale metre subtraction: an f32 longitude has 2.4e-7 rad = 1.5 m of precision against 250 m cells, and an f32 `(I + 0.5) * cell_rad_0` with `I` up to 160,120 carries about 4 m. Rust computes the ground cell in f64 from `p_l` and writes exact integers to the pad. The footprint in the march is `foot = max(tm * pix_ang, dt * 0.25)` in p-units and `lodb = log2(foot / g_cloud_upkm)`, unchanged FOR THE NOISE MIP; the PROFILE's hand-off weight and tap level read the pixel footprint alone, `lodb_pix = log2(max(tm * pix_ang / g_cloud_upkm, 1e-4))` (E1 lever 1, v0.1294): the `dt` term is the march's own step, which grows as 1 / cos on oblique rays and is uncapped on a clear-air stride, so it fired the profile on samples whose pixel never handed off and broke G7 bit-exactness below the band (52 percent of pixels at the 30 km rung). Rust's `pix_ang_march` for the active-level range is `2 tan(fov/2) / (config.height / cloud_res_div)`, the value the temporal march derives from its own rasterizer, and `2 tan(fov/2) / config.height` for the shell path.
