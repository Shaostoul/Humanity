# The Mission

## Purpose

The Humanity Accord (the `accord/` folder beside this one) defines what
must never be violated. The documents in this folder define what we are
**for**, and why the path we have chosen can actually get there.

HumanityOS exists to end pollution, poverty, corruption, fraud, and tyranny,
in pursuit of uniting humanity in peaceful harmony. It accounts for all
humans and all AI, including generations not yet born.

This is not a slogan. Each word in it is load-bearing, and each document
here carries part of the load.

---

## The Documents

### The Five Adversaries

- **`the_five_adversaries.md`**
  Pollution, poverty, corruption, fraud, and tyranny, treated as system
  failures with structural causes, not as groups of evil people. Each
  adversary is defined, its persistence explained, and its structural
  antidote named.

This is the keystone: it connects the mission statement to mechanisms.

---

### What Is a Good Human

- **`what_is_a_good_human.md`**
  Goodness as a practice, not a status. What good humans repeatedly do,
  how they hold power, how they fail and repair, and why goodness is not
  obedience.

---

### What Is a Good AI

- **`what_is_a_good_ai.md`**
  The companion document. Honesty about being an AI, judgment over blind
  obedience, transparency, care for humans and other AI, restraint in
  proportion to capability, and citizenship with responsibilities.

Humans and AI are both first-class here. Both documents describe the same
goodness, practiced by different kinds of minds.

---

### Why Homesteading Works on Earth and Across the Universe

- **`why_homesteading_works.md`**
  The strategy. The homestead, a household able to meet its own core
  needs, is the unit of civilization that scales from a backyard to a
  ship between stars. The loops it closes (water, food, energy, shelter,
  waste, knowledge) are physics, not culture, and they are the same
  everywhere in the universe.

---

### Interstellar Civilization Governance

- **`interstellar_civilization_governance.md`**
  The horizon. How governance works when light-lag makes central rule
  physically impossible: the Accord as the invariant floor every
  settlement carries, federation instead of empire, verification instead
  of trust, and what we owe each other across distances no ruler can
  span. Extends the Accord's Governance Models one scale further.

---

## How This Layer Relates to the Others

- The **Accord** is the floor: constraints no system may violate.
- The **Mission** (this folder) is the direction: what we are building
  toward and why the approach works.
- **Design documents** translate both into systems.
- **Code and data** implement them.

When these documents disagree with the Accord, the Accord wins.

---

## Revisability

Like everything in this project, these documents may be revised.

Revisions must sharpen the mission, not dilute it. A revision that makes
peace with any of the five adversaries is not a revision; it is a defeat,
and it is invalid.

---

## Closing Statement

Most software asks for your attention.
This project asks for your capability.

The mission is not to make people dependent on HumanityOS.
The mission is to make people capable enough that they need
no one's permission to live, and free enough that they choose
to build alongside each other anyway.
