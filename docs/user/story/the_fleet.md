# The Fleet

*The story the game is set in. What in it is made up, and what is real, is on the page
[What Is Made Up in This Game](what_is_made_up_in_this_game.md).*

It is centuries from now, and humanity is one people.

We did not get here by beating each other. What kept people poor and afraid was never other
people. It was pollution, poverty, corruption, fraud and tyranny, and we ended them the way you fix
a machine: by building ways of living in which they stopped paying. People still disagree. We
settle it by talking and by voting, not by fighting. Peaceful harmony is not the absence of
conflict.

Earth is healthy. Nobody had to leave. We left anyway, the way people always have: to see what is
out there, and to learn to live well anywhere.

## The fleet

The fleet is that leaving. Its motherships are cities flying between the stars, each one home to
more people than most countries, with transports, freighters and scouts running between them. A
mothership crosses from one place to the next in a jump, and nothing pushes on it while it
travels. Its great drums turn, and at their rims you weigh what you weighed on Earth.

Between the stars there is no sun, no soil and no shop. Everything a person needs, the fleet makes
for itself: air, water, food, power, tools and homes. That makes the fleet the biggest experiment
in living well that people have ever run.

Every home aboard is a real home: an Earth homestead under Earth's weight, on Earth's day, growing
Earth's crops in real soil, with real water, real power and real repairs. What works is kept. What
fails is fixed. The best of it goes two ways: ahead, to the worlds the fleet will settle, and back
home to Earth, where people build the same designs on the same kind of land.

So your home matters. What you learn keeping it alive is not made up. The plants, the numbers and
the skills are real, and you can use them where you really live.

## You are crew

You are crew, and nobody aboard goes without. Help in whatever way you enjoy: grow food, build,
fix, haul, cook, teach, explore, look after people. There is no wrong way to help.

You came aboard as a volunteer of Project Universe, the programme that brings new crew to the
fleet. The mothership you are joining is the UHS Kintsugi. UHS stands for United Humanity Ship.
Kintsugi is the Japanese art of mending broken pottery with gold, so that the repair becomes the
most beautiful part of the bowl. The ship's motto is *Ne Obliviscaris*: "forget not". Remember
Earth, the people who came before us, and why we left. (The crew may one day vote the ship a new
name. Until then, it is the Kintsugi.)

## Crew live long

When you come aboard you are joined to the lifeline, and from then on your mind runs on your brain
and on the lifeline together. If your body dies, you do not: you fall asleep, and you wake in a new
body grown from the genes you were born with. You keep everything you know. You lose everything
physical: what was added to your body, and, if the world you play in is set that way, what you
were carrying. The page [Coming Back as Yourself](coming_back_as_yourself.md) says how that works,
and why it is still you.

The lifeline carries your mind. What else a person is, the fleet leaves to each person.

The lifeline has an edge. Past it, go with friends, and take a field lifeline: a heavy machine,
carried like a backpack, that takes the lifeline out there with your team.

## Today you arrive

A transport is bringing you in with a dozen other new crew and a hold full of cargo. Welcome
aboard.

## Where the game is today

The story's present is between the stars. The game is not there yet: for now the ship you play on
waits in orbit above Earth, and you can see Earth below it. The trip out between the stars, the
lifeline's edge and the arrival on a transport are still being built.
