# What Is Made Up in This Game

*A teaching game must never teach a made-up thing as a fact. This page lists what in HumanityOS is
made up, so you always know which is which. The story itself is on the page [The Fleet](the_fleet.md).*

## Made up for the story

1. **A mind running on a machine as well as on a brain.** The lifeline, the field lifeline and
   waking in a new body all rest on this. Nobody knows how to do it, or whether it can be done.
2. **The lifeline's reach.** Two supports of one mind would have to stay in close touch, and a
   signal at the speed of light would not even reach the Moon in time. The lifeline reaches a
   light-year, a distance light takes a whole year to cross. Nothing we know of can do that.
3. **The jump.** Craft and motherships in the game cross from one place to another without
   travelling the space between. Nothing we know of can do that either. Real ships between the
   stars would take years or lifetimes.
4. **How fast a respawner works.** It grows an adult body in moments. A real body takes about two
   decades.

## Made up in play

5. **Magic.** Spells such as Fireball, Ice Bolt, Heal and Telekinesis are fantasy. They are marked
   with the word "fantasy" where your abilities are listed, on the Profile page.
6. **The fleet's own inventions.** Abilities such as Nano Heal and Energy Shield are made up too,
   even though they sound like technology. Nothing real heals a wound or stops a blow that way.

## Everything else is as real as we can make it

The crops, the water, the air, the power, the food and what it does to your body, the weight you
feel at the rim of a spinning drum, the distances between the stars, and the grain of dust that
hits like a bomb at a starship's speed: all of these follow the real numbers, from sources we can
point to.

Where the game shows a real skill (growing a tomato, cleaning water, treating a burn), it is meant
to be something you can do where you really live.

If you find something on the real side that is wrong, please tell us on the Bugs page. A wrong fact
in a teaching game is a bug.

## Why this page exists

The project says plainly what it will not build and why, and it says just as plainly what it made
up. That way nobody has to guess which parts of the game they can trust in real life.
